Sometimes one customer ends up as two contacts, for example one from WhatsApp and one from email. Merging joins them into one contact with one chat history.

## When you see a merge offer

When the app is not sure two contacts are the same person, it asks you first. You meet a merge offer in two places:

1. **On a contact profile.** You save a changed phone number, and the app finds another contact with a matching number.
2. **In a web chat.** A bar called **Possible duplicate** appears, with **Merge** and **Dismiss**. See [Web chat conversations](/help/inbox/web-chat-conversations).

There is no separate Merge button on the contact list.

## Merge from a contact profile

Before you start, decide which contact you want to keep. The contact you open and edit is the one that stays. The other contact is folded into it and then deleted.

1. Click **Contacts**, then click the contact you want to keep.
2. On the **Details** tab, correct the **Phone**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.
4. If another contact has a matching number, a window called **Merge with existing contact?** opens. It names the other contact. Read the name.
5. Click **Merge** to join them, or **Keep separate** to leave both as they are.
6. Wait for the message **Contacts merged**.

> [!WARNING]
> A merge cannot be undone. The other contact is deleted after its details move across. Check the name in the window before you click **Merge**.

## What is kept

The contact you kept receives:

- all chats and messages from the other contact, joined into one chat history
- the other contact's deals, broadcast history, tags and notes
- the other contact's custom field values, when your contact has none for that field
- the other contact's channel links (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, web chat and email) that your contact did not already have
- the other contact's name, email, company and picture, only when your contact has none

Where both contacts have a value, your contact keeps its own.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Tickets are not on the list of things that move. Before you merge, open the other contact and check its ticket history. If it has tickets you need, ask your admin first.

## When the phone number is refused

One phone number can belong to only one contact. If you change a contact's phone to a number another contact already has, the app does not save it. It names the other contact instead, in a window called **This number belongs to another contact** (or a red notice in the **Edit Contact** window).

- If you have the "Merge contacts" permission, click **Merge and save number** in that window, or **Merge these contacts** in the Edit window and then **Merge** to confirm. The other contact's history moves into the one you are editing, the other contact is deleted, and your number is saved. In the Edit window, click **Update** afterwards to save the number.
- If you do not have the permission, ask a colleague who does, or use a different number.

If the two are not the same person, click **Keep separate** and choose another number.

## Who can merge

By default Agents can merge contacts. If the **Merge** button is greyed out, your role does not have the "Merge contacts" permission. Ask your admin. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- Merge into the contact that has the longest chat history, so the important messages stay in place.
- After a merge, open the kept contact and check its tags and notes.
- Use **Keep separate** if you are not sure. You can always merge later.

## Common mistakes

- Merging into the wrong contact. The one you opened is kept.
- Forgetting the other contact's tickets.
- Clicking **Merge** for two people who share a phone, such as a family. Check the names.
