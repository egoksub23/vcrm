A contact is one customer. Every chat, ticket and deal belongs to a contact, so correct details here make the rest of your work easier.

## Open the contact list

Click **Contacts** in the sidebar. Each row shows Name, Phone, Email, Company, Tags and the date the contact was Created. On a small screen some columns are hidden. The list shows 25 contacts per page. Use the arrows under the list to move between pages.

![Contacts list with the search box, the tag filter and one row for each contact.](/help/img/contacts-overview-01-list.png)

## Find a contact

1. Type in the box that says **Search by name, phone, or email...**.
2. To narrow the list by tag, click **Filter by tags** and tick one or more tags. You see contacts that have any of the tags you ticked.
3. Click **Clear all** to remove the tag filter.

The search does not look at the Company column. It matches a phone number exactly as it was typed when the contact was saved, so if a number is not found, try fewer digits.

## Read a profile

Click any row. A panel opens on the right. At the top you see the name, the phone number (click it to copy), the email and the company. Two buttons sit below:

- **Send template** picks a WhatsApp template and sends it to this contact straight away, so check the preview first.
- **New Ticket** raises a ticket for this contact. See [Create a ticket](/help/tickets/create-a-ticket).

![Contact profile panel with the Details tab open.](/help/img/contacts-overview-02-profile.png)

The tabs are:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Change the name, phone, email or company, then click **Save Changes**. The phone number is required. |
| **Tags** | Click a tag to add it or remove it. It saves at once. |
| **Notes** | Write in **Write a note...** and click **Add Note**. Point at a note to show the bin icon that deletes it. |
| **Custom Fields** | Fill in extra fields your admin created, then click **Save Custom Fields**. |
| **Deals** | See the contact's deals. You change deals on the Pipelines page. |

The notes on this tab are the same notes you see as **Session notes** beside a chat.

## Country, language and lifecycle stage

- **Country and Language** are set in the Inbox. Open a chat, then use the **Contact fields** card in the right-hand column. Click a value to edit it. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click away to save, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. The two pickers save as soon as you choose. The Language is the customer's preferred conversation language, and AI drafts and replies use it.
- **Lifecycle stage** is Lead, Active, Customer or Churned. Change it in the **Edit Contact** window: click the three dots at the end of a row, then **Edit**. The Lifecycle report shows how many contacts are in each stage.

## Add a contact

1. Click **Add Contact**.
2. Type the **Name**. It is optional. Only the phone number is required.
3. Type the **Phone Number**, including the country code (for example +60 for Malaysia). The phone number is marked with a red star. The app will not save the contact without it.
4. Add the email and company if you know them.
5. Choose the **Lifecycle stage**. New contacts start as Lead.
6. Click the tags that apply.
7. Click **Create**.

When you leave the phone box, the app checks for the same number. A red notice, for example "Jane Doe already has this phone number.", names the contact that owns it and stops you from creating a copy. Click **View** in that notice to open the existing contact. An amber notice, "A contact with a very similar number already exists.", is a warning. Check it before you go on.

## Delete a contact

Click the three dots at the end of a row, then **Delete**. You can also tick several rows and click **Delete selected**. Ticking works on the current page only.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting a contact cannot be undone. It also removes that person's chats with all their messages, and their tickets. Deals stay, but they no longer show a contact.

## Who can do what

By default an Agent can add, edit, import, export and delete contacts. The **Custom fields** button on this page is for admins only. A Viewer can read contacts but cannot change them. If a button is greyed out, hover over it to read why, and see [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- Add the country code to every phone number so WhatsApp can reach the customer.
- Use tags for who the customer is, such as "VIP". Use conversation labels for what a chat is about. See [Tags, labels and priority](/help/inbox/tags-labels-priority).
- Check the search box before you add a contact. Two contacts for one person make chats hard to follow.

## Common mistakes

- Searching by company name. Search only looks at name, phone and email.
- Leaving a tag filter switched on, then wondering why contacts are missing. Click **Clear all**.
- Deleting a contact to fix a typo. Edit the contact instead.
