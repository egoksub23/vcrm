A deal is a possible sale to one contact. A pipeline is a board with a column for each step of your sale. You move a deal from column to column as the sale moves forward.

## Open the board

Click **Pipelines** in the sidebar. Each column is a stage, and each card is a deal. A new pipeline starts with the stages New Lead, Qualified, Proposal Sent, Negotiation and Won. Your admin can change the stages.

At the top left, click the pipeline name to switch to another pipeline. Above the board, six tiles give totals: Total Deals, Pipeline Value, Avg Deal Size, Weighted Value, Won This Month and Lost This Month. Each tile has a short explanation of how the number is worked out.

![The pipeline board with stage columns, deal cards and the summary tiles.](/help/img/pipelines-and-deals-01-board.png)

## Add a deal

1. Click **Add Deal**. To start in a certain stage, click **Add Deal** at the bottom of that column instead.
2. Type a **Title**.
3. Choose the **Contact**. If that contact has a chat, a **Link to Conversation** shortcut appears. It opens the Inbox.
4. Type the **Value** and choose the **Currency**.
5. Set the **Expected Close Date** if you know it.
6. Check the **Stage**. Choose who the deal is **Assigned To**, or leave it Unassigned.
7. Add **Notes** if you like.
8. Click **Create Deal**.

The Title, Contact and Stage are required.

## Move a deal

Drag the card to another column. It saves when you drop it. If the move fails, you see "Failed to move deal" and the card goes back.

To use the keyboard, focus a card, press <kbd>Space</kbd> to pick it up, use the arrow keys to move it, then press <kbd>Space</kbd> to drop it. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel.

## Edit, win or lose a deal

Click a deal card to open **Edit Deal**. Change any field and click **Save Changes**.

At the bottom, the Status area has **Mark as Won** and **Mark as Lost**. Once a deal is marked, a Won or Lost badge shows on the card and **Reopen deal** appears. Marking a deal does not move the card to another column. Lost deals are left out of the deal count and the value tiles.

To remove a deal, click **Delete Deal**, then **Confirm** when the app asks "Delete this deal?".

## Deals and chats

There is no button inside a chat to create a deal. Create it on the Pipelines page. In the Inbox, the right-hand column shows the contact's deals under **Deals**. On the contact profile, the **Deals** tab lists them too, and you can see the stage and value there.

## What you can and cannot do

By default an Agent can add, edit, move, win, lose and delete deals. Adding a pipeline, and managing pipelines and stages (the **Add Pipeline** button and **Manage Pipelines** in the menu), are for admins. If a button is greyed out, hover over it to read why. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- Give each deal a clear title, such as the customer and the product.
- Set an expected close date, so you can see which deals are close.
- Write what the customer said in **Notes**, so a teammate can carry on.

## Common mistakes

- Marking a deal Won but leaving the card in an early stage. Drag it too.
- Choosing the wrong contact when two contacts share a name. Check the phone number on the profile first.
- Looking for a deal after its contact was deleted. The deal stays, but it shows no contact.
