Coaches report from the coach portal. When a coach taps **Report incident**, the portal opens a short, phone-friendly form: who was involved (picked from that activity's student list), what happened, how serious it was, and photos. The coach does not need a login here.

## What your school sees

- The incident appears on the Dashboard like any other, marked as coming from the coach portal.
- The **Report answers** panel shows the coach's name and email, the activity, the students named, and every answer, under the version of the form that was used.
- Your rules notify the right roles in the same way. The **Confirm to the reporter** rule can send the coach a confirmation.
- Photos can be added for 30 minutes after sending, up to 5 files of 10 MB each.
- The form works in English, Malay and Chinese, and keeps a draft if the connection drops.

## What the portal sends

For every report the portal sends the school's **Vircle Group ID**, the **coach's name and email** (both required), the activity, and the list of students. The coach's name and email are recorded as the person who reported the incident, and any reply goes to that email.

## Who manages the connection

The connection is managed by the Halo platform administrator, not by each school: the key, which Vircle Group belongs to which school, and the web addresses allowed to show the form. Your school does not see these settings.

If reports from coaches are not arriving, ask your administrator to contact the platform administrator. Typical causes are that the group is not linked to your school, or the portal is not sending the coach's email.

> [!NOTE]
> A test report is marked **TEST**, tells only your administrators, and is left out of the trends.

## Change the questions

Your school can change the questions a coach sees under **Setup, Report form**. See [Set up Incidents](/help/incidents/set-up-incidents).
