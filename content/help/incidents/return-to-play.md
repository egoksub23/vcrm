When a student is injured, the school nurse can set the first day the student may play again. The coach portal shows a warning beside the student until that day.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A return-to-play date is a warning, not a lock. Nothing stops a student from joining anything. It makes sure the coach knows.

You need the **medical** permission. By default Administrators and Owners have it, and your school can give it to the nurse's role under Settings, Roles & permissions.

## Set a date

1. Open the incident. The **Return to play** panel lists the students named on it. These are students who came from the coach portal's list.
2. Choose the **Type**: injury, concussion, illness or other.
3. Choose the **Not before** date.
4. Click **Save date**. The panel then shows "Warning until" and the date.

Setting a date again for the same student on the same incident replaces the earlier one.

## See everyone who is restricted

On the Dashboard, choose the **Return to play** view. It lists each student with a date still in force, the type, the date, and the incident. Click **Clear now** when a student may play early. The record stays, with the time it was cleared.

## Tips

- Use a longer date for a suspected concussion.
- Clear a date as soon as the student is fit, so the warning does not outlast the injury.
