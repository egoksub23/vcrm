Incidents is where your school records anything that goes wrong, or nearly does: an injury at sports practice, a theft, damaged property, a data leak, a safeguarding concern. Each incident gets a reference such as **INC-2026-014**, a severity, a lead, a list of required steps with deadlines, and a timeline, so you can show later what happened and what was done.

It is separate from Tickets (customer follow-ups) and from Sembang (your team's chat). Use Incidents when something needs a formal record.

> [!NOTE]
> Incidents records and tracks, and tells the right people. It does not notify the police, the education office, the ministry, the PDP Commissioner or parents on its own. A person still sends those, and you can [log that it was done](/help/incidents/manage-escalate-and-close).

## Who can see and do what

- **Anyone** can raise an incident. Reporting is open on purpose, so nobody hesitates because they are unsure they are allowed.
- A reporter sees the incidents they raised or are watching.
- **Administrators and Owners** see every incident, change them, and see Reports and Setup. A school can give these permissions to other roles under Settings, Roles & permissions.
- If you cannot see **Incidents** in the sidebar, it may not be switched on for your school. Ask your administrator.

## The Dashboard

Click **Incidents** in the sidebar. The Dashboard is one page for everything.

![The Incidents Dashboard with four number tiles, a row of filters and the Board view](/help/img/incidents-overview-01-board.png)

### The four numbers

Each tile is also a filter. Click one to show only those incidents; click it again to switch it off. You can combine them.

| Tile | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Open incidents** | Every incident that is not Closed. |
| **Need action now** | Incidents nobody has acknowledged, with an overdue step, or serious and still open. |
| **Required steps overdue** | Incidents with a required step past its deadline. |
| **Not yet acknowledged** | Incidents still at Reported where nobody has confirmed they have seen the notice. |

### Filters

| Filter | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Find an incident by title or reference, for example INC-2026-14. |
| **Mine** | Show incidents you lead or reported. |
| **Status**, **Category**, **Severity** | Tick one or more values. |
| **Involves** | Show incidents that involve, for example, an injury or personal data. |
| **Lead** | Show one person's incidents, or only those with nobody leading. |
| **Student name** | Find incidents that name a student (only incidents you can open are searched). |
| **From** and **To** | Limit by the date the incident was raised. |

A line under the filters says how many you are looking at, for example "Showing 3 of 12". Click **Clear filters** to start again. The filters are kept in the web address, so you can bookmark a view or send the link to a colleague. The Dashboard loads the newest 200 incidents; for older ones use [Reports and registers](/help/incidents/reports-and-registers).

### Board, List and Return to play

- **Board**: one column per status. Drag a card to change its status.
- **List**: a table with key, title, type, severity, status, lead and age.
- **Return to play**: only for the school nurse. See [Return to play](/help/incidents/return-to-play).

## Severities

The school template uses four levels.

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Needs attention right now. |
| **High** | Urgent, same-day attention. |
| **Medium** | Attend to it in the normal course of work. |
| **Low** | Minor, low impact. |

## Statuses

| Status | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Reported** | Just raised, not yet looked at. |
| **Triaged** | Someone has looked at it, classified it and set a lead. |
| **Contained** | The immediate risk is under control. |
| **Investigating** | The cause is being worked out. |
| **Recovered** | Things are back to normal. |
| **Closed** | Finished. Everything stays on the record. |

## Tips

- Report early. A minor report costs a minute; an unreported incident can cost much more.
- If you are unsure how serious something is, raise it anyway and let the incident team decide.
- Open **My tasks** each morning to see what you personally owe.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting until you are sure it is serious.** Raise it as soon as you notice, then add notes.
- **Using Incidents for a customer complaint.** That belongs in a Ticket.
- **Expecting to see every incident.** Unless you manage incidents, you only see the ones you raised or watch.
- **Looking for an old incident on the Dashboard.** It shows the newest 200. Use Reports, Registers with a date range.
