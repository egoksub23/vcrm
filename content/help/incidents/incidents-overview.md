Incidents is Vircle's Security Incident Register — a place to report, triage and track security, fraud and data incidents through to closure. It is separate from Tickets (customer follow-ups) and from Sembang (your team's chat): use Incidents when something needs a formal record for compliance, not a general to-do.

Each incident gets a key such as **INC-2026-014**, so you and your team can talk about the same one.

> [!NOTE]
> Incidents digitises your workspace's internal register and workflow. It does not itself notify Bank Negara Malaysia, your sponsor e-money issuer, the PDP Commissioner or anyone else outside the company — a person still sends that through the proper channel. See [Log external notifications](/help/incidents/manage-escalate-and-close#log-external-notifications).

## Who can see and use Incidents

- **Anyone** can raise an incident — reporting is open on purpose, so nobody hesitates because they are not sure they are "allowed" to flag something.
- A reporter only sees the incidents they raised or are watching.
- A **Compliance Officer**, Admin or Owner sees the whole register. See [Manage, escalate and close an incident](/help/incidents/manage-escalate-and-close#the-compliance-officer-role).

## Open the Incidents page

Click **Incidents** in the sidebar, next to Sembang. At the top right, switch between **Board** and **List**, the same way Tickets does.

## Severities

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P1 · Critical** | Needs attention right now. |
| **P2 · High** | Urgent, same-day attention. |
| **P3 · Medium** | The default — attend to it in the normal course of work. |
| **P4 · Low** | Minor, low-impact. |

Some kinds of report (for example a suspected data breach, a float/safeguarding issue, an account takeover, or anything reported by a regulator or partner) are usually classified at least **P2** by policy. The Raise Incident form flags this while you are reporting, but a Compliance Officer makes the final call during triage — including downgrading it, with a reason on record.

## Statuses

| Status | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Reported** | Just raised, not yet triaged. |
| **Triaged** | A Compliance Officer has classified it and set an owner. |
| **Contained** | The immediate risk is under control. |
| **Investigating** | Root cause work is under way. |
| **Recovered** | Systems and data are back to normal. |
| **Closed** | Finished, with a root cause on record. |

## Board and list view

The board has one column per status, same drag-to-move interaction as Tickets. The list shows Key, Title, Type, Severity, Status, Lead and Age, and you can search across both by key or title.

## Tips

- Report early and report often — a P4 that turns out to be nothing costs a minute of everyone's time; a real incident nobody reported costs much more.
- If you are not sure how serious something is, raise it anyway and let Compliance decide the severity.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting until you're sure it's serious before raising it.** Raise it as soon as you notice something — severity is Compliance's job, not yours.
- **Using Incidents for a customer complaint.** That belongs in a Ticket. Incidents is for security, fraud and data events.
- **Expecting to see every incident.** Unless you are a Compliance Officer, Admin or Owner, you only see the ones you raised or are watching.
