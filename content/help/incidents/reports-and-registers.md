**Incidents, Reports** is for people who manage incidents. It has two tabs.

## Trends

Covers the last 12 months. Test reports are left out. It starts with what to do, then the numbers.

**Needs your attention.** A short list in plain words, each with a **See them** link that opens the Dashboard on exactly those incidents:

- incidents with a required step past its deadline
- incidents nobody has acknowledged
- serious incidents that are still open
- incidents open for 14 days or more
- a student named on two or more incidents in 90 days
- a place with three or more incidents in 90 days
- raising that is rising: at least three in the last 30 days and half as many again as the 30 before

When none of these applies, the box says so.

**The numbers.** Raised in the last 30 days (against the 30 before), still open, closed, and the typical time to close (the median, so one very old incident does not skew it).

**Where and when.** What kind, how serious, which places, and per month. Each bar is a share of all incidents, so one incident out of five reads as a fifth. Categories use the names from the template each incident was raised under. Months start from the first one with an incident.

## Registers

A register is a list of incidents of one kind, for an auditor, school leadership or the data protection officer.

| Register | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Injuries** | Incidents that involve an injury. |
| **Data and security breaches** | Incidents involving personal data, computer systems or a security breach. |
| **Near misses** | Mishaps where nobody was hurt. |
| **Property damage** | Incidents involving damaged or lost property. |
| **Child welfare concerns** | Safeguarding concerns. |

An incident appears in every register it belongs to. Pick a register and a date range. Click **Download CSV** to export it; you need the export permission. Test reports are listed and marked **Test**. The CSV is safe to open in Excel.

## Tips

- Run the near-miss register every term. Near misses are the cheapest warning you will get.
- Export a register before an audit so you are not collecting records at the last minute.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for old incidents on the Dashboard.** It shows the newest 200. Use a register with a date range.
- **Expecting a test report in the trends.** Test reports are left out on purpose.
