**Incidents, Reports** is for people who manage incidents. It has two tabs.

## Trends

Covers the last 12 months. Test reports are left out.

- The total number of incidents.
- **By month, category, severity and activity** (the top 10), shown as bars.
- **Places where it keeps happening**: three or more incidents at the same place within 90 days. Worth a look before the next one.

## Registers

A register is a list of incidents of one kind, for an auditor, the education office or the data protection officer.

| Register | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Injuries** | Incidents that involve an injury. |
| **Data and security breaches** | Incidents involving personal data, computer systems or a security breach. |
| **Near misses** | Mishaps where nobody was hurt. |
| **Property damage** | Incidents involving damaged or lost property. |
| **Child welfare concerns** | Safeguarding concerns. |

An incident appears in every register it belongs to. Pick a register and a date range. Click **Download CSV** to export it; you need the export permission. Test reports are listed and marked **Test**. The CSV is safe to open in Excel.

## Tips

- Run the near-miss register every term. Near misses are the cheapest warning you will get.
- Export a register before an audit so you are not collecting records at the last minute.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for old incidents on the Dashboard.** It shows the newest 200. Use a register with a date range.
- **Expecting a test report in the trends.** Test reports are left out on purpose.
