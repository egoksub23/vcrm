Raise a ticket when a problem cannot be solved in the chat and someone must follow it up. It takes about a minute.

## Choose where to start

| Start here | What is filled in for you |
|---|---|
| A chat: click **Raise Ticket** in the chat header | The contact, and a link back to this chat. |
| A contact profile: click **New Ticket** | The contact. There is no link to a chat. |
| Beside a chat, in **Ticket history**: click **New** | The contact, and a link back to that chat. |
| The **Tickets** page: click **New Ticket** | Nothing. You choose the contact. |

If a chat is linked, the ticket has an **Open conversation** link that takes you back to it.

## Create the ticket

1. Open the form using one of the ways above. The window is called **New Ticket**.
2. Choose the **Type**: Bug, Feature request, Technical, Billing, Account, General or Other. It starts as General.
3. If you started from the Tickets page, search for the contact by name, phone or email and click the right one. Click **Change** to pick another person.
4. Type the **Summary**. This is required. Keep it short and clear.
5. Add a **Description** if more detail helps. It is optional.
6. Choose the **Assignee**. Click **Assign to me** if you will do the work.
7. Choose the **Team**, if your workspace has teams.
8. Choose the **Priority**: Urgent, High, Normal or Low. It starts as Normal.
9. Add **Labels**. Click **Add label**, type a label and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Labels your team used before are suggested. A ticket can have up to 10 labels.
10. Set a **Due date** if there is one.
11. Add **Attachments**. See below.
12. Fill in any extra fields you see (see below).
13. Click **Create Ticket**.

You see a message such as "VIR-12 created" with an **Open** button. The ticket also appears on the Tickets page.

![The New Ticket window with the type, summary, description and routing fields.](/help/img/create-a-ticket-01-dialog.png)

## Extra fields on the form

Your admin can add their own fields to this form. A field can be short text, long text, a number, a date, a dropdown or a checkbox. Fields marked as required must be filled in, or you see "Fill in the required fields". Some fields only appear for certain ticket types, so change the **Type** first and then look again.

## Attachments

Click **Add files**, drop files into the window, or paste a picture. A ticket can have up to 20 files. A picture can be up to 5 MB and other files up to 16 MB. If a file fails, the ticket is still created, and you see how many files could not be uploaded.

## Create several in a row

Tick **Create another** before you click **Create Ticket**. The window stays open. It keeps the contact, the type and the routing fields (assignee, team and priority) and clears the summary, description, labels, due date and extra fields.

## What you can do

By default an Agent can raise tickets. If **New Ticket** or **Raise Ticket** is greyed out, your role is read-only for tickets. Ask your admin. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- Write the summary so that someone who was not in the chat can understand it.
- Raise the ticket from the chat when you can. The link back saves your teammate time.
- Choose an assignee, or the ticket may wait for a long time without an owner.

## Common mistakes

- Raising a ticket from the contact profile when the problem is in an open chat. Use **Raise Ticket** in the chat so the two are linked.
- Leaving the type as General when it is a Billing or Bug problem. The type is used in filters and reports.
