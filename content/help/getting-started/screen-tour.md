This page walks through the parts of Vircle CRM that are always on screen, and what each sidebar item is for.

> [!NOTE]
> You only see the sidebar items your role includes. If an item below is missing for you, that is normal. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## The sidebar

The sidebar is on the left. On a phone or a small screen it is hidden. Press the menu button (three lines) in the top left corner to open it.

On a computer, the small double-arrow button at the top of the sidebar switches between **Collapse sidebar** (icons only; the sidebar opens while you point at it) and **Pin sidebar open** (always wide). Your choice is remembered on this device.

![The left sidebar with all items, from Dashboard down to Settings](/help/img/screen-tour-01-sidebar.png)

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Numbers and charts for the whole team. Also where you land after you sign in. |
| **Inbox** | Chats, emails and public comments from customers. This is where agents spend most of the day. A pulsing dot means there are unread chats. |
| **Notifications** | Your alerts: chats assigned to you, mentions, ticket updates and more. A number shows how many are unread. |
| **Contacts** | Your customer records, with tags, notes and deals. |
| **Pipelines** | Sales deals as cards on a board, moved between stages. |
| **Broadcasts** | Messages sent to many contacts at once. |
| **Tickets** | Longer problems that need tracking, with a key such as VIR-12. |
| **Automations** | Rules that run by themselves. Designed by admins. |
| **Flows** | Guided conversation flows. Marked **Beta**. Designed by admins. |
| **Knowledge** | Answers written by your team. You can insert them into chats. |
| **AI Agents** | Setup for the AI assistant. Done by admins. |
| **Reports** | Charts and tables about chats, tickets and the team. |
| **Settings** | Your profile, appearance and, for admins, the workspace setup. Below a divider line. |
| **User Guide** | This guide. |

If a badge with a number is on **Settings**, proposals are waiting for a decision. Only people who can approve see it.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard is a live summary. From top to bottom:

- Four cards: **Active Conversations**, **New Contacts Today**, **Open Deals Value** and **Messages Sent Today**.
- Shortcut buttons: **New Contact**, **New Deal**, **New Broadcast** and **New Automation**. They open the page where that is done.
- **Conversations Over Time**, a chart of incoming and outgoing messages. You can switch the range to 7, 30 or 90 days.
- **Pipeline Value**, a chart of open deals by stage.
- **Average First Response Time**, a chart of how many minutes it takes to reply to a customer's first unreplied message, by weekday.
- **Recent Activity**, a list of what just happened across chats, deals, broadcasts and automations.

![The Dashboard with the four number cards, the charts and the Recent Activity list](/help/img/screen-tour-02-dashboard.png)

Until your team has some activity, the charts and the list show an empty message instead, for example "No activity yet".

## The header

The header is the strip along the top of every page.

- The page name is on the left.
- The **sun or moon button** switches between light and dark mode.
- Your **photo or initial** opens the account menu.

## The account menu

The account menu shows your name and email, then:

- **Profile**: your photo, name, email and browser notifications.
- **Settings**: the Settings page.
- **Sign out**.

**Profile** and **Settings** only appear if your role includes the Settings page. **Sign out** is always there. The same menu is at the bottom of the sidebar, where your name and email are shown.

## Tips

- On a phone, the Inbox shows one panel at a time. Tap a chat to open it, and tap the back arrow to return to the list.
- Deep links work. If a teammate sends you a link to a chat, it opens that chat once you are signed in.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for something in Settings that is not there.** Most workspace setup is for admins. Ask your admin.
- **Thinking a missing sidebar item is a fault.** Your admin chooses which items each role sees.
