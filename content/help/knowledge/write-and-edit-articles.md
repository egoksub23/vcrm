A good article lets a teammate, or the AI, answer a customer correctly without asking anyone. This page is for people who are allowed to write articles.

## Before you start

By default an Agent can write articles and edit their own drafts. When you save, the article is a **draft**. An admin reviews and publishes it, so it cannot reach a customer before someone checks it. If a page says "You can read this article but not change it", you cannot edit that one. Agents can edit their own drafts, and admins can edit everything. If you see "You can't add articles", ask your admin. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Write an article

1. Click **Knowledge**, then **Add content**. Under **Write directly**, click **Article** or **Q&A** — either opens the editor with that kind already selected. You can also start from a chat message. See [Use articles in a chat](/help/knowledge/use-articles-in-chat).
2. If you want to switch kind after opening, the toggle at the top of the editor still lets you choose **Article** for free text, or **Question & answer**.
3. Type the **Title**. For a question and answer, type the **Question**.
4. Write the **Content**, or the **Answer**. Write it the way you would explain it to a customer. Include the exact prices, dates and steps.
5. In the settings column, choose a **Collection**, and check the **Language**.
6. Set **Review by** to a date when someone should check the article again.
7. Switch **Let the AI use this article** off if it is for agents only.
8. Click **Save draft**.

![The article editor with the formatting toolbar, the attachments area and the settings column.](/help/img/write-and-edit-articles-01-editor.png)

Admins also see **Publish**. When you save as an agent, you see: Saved as a draft for an admin to review.

## Format the text

The toolbar has Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Heading, Subheading, Bulleted list, Numbered list, Quote, Link and Insert image, and an emoji button. For a link, type a web address, an email address or a phone link. Anything else is refused. One article can hold up to 20,000 characters. A counter appears when you get close.

## Pictures and files

- **Paste or drop a picture** into the text. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> or drop the file. It uploads in the background and is made smaller if it is large. You can add an **Image caption**. Customers see the caption with the picture. Only PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF pictures are allowed.
- **Attach files** under **Attachments**: drag files there, or click **or choose files**. An article can have up to 10 files. Pictures can be up to 5 MB and other files up to 16 MB.
- Each file has **Send with AI answers**. Switch it off if only agents should send that file.

## Test it

The **Test it: would the AI find this?** box in the settings column checks your article. Type a question a customer might ask and click **Test**. It shows whether this article would be sent to the AI, or matched only below the cut-off, or did not come up. The test searches the saved article, so save first. If it says the article did not come up, add the customer's own words to your text.

## Go back to an earlier version

Click **Edit history**. You see earlier saved versions. Click **Restore** on the one you want. The current text is kept in the history, but unsaved edits in the editor are lost.

## Bring content in

Click **Add content** on the library page. Under **Bring content in** you have three more choices. Everything you import lands as a draft, and nothing goes live until an admin publishes it.

- **Bulk import Q&A (CSV)**: paste one `Question | Answer` per line, or click **Upload a CSV** with columns named `question` and `answer`. Up to 200 pairs are imported at a time.
- **Upload a file**: text, Markdown, CSV, Word or PDF, up to 10 MB each. A long document is split into several draft articles, up to 20.
- **Import a web page**: paste the address of a public page that starts with `http://` or `https://`. The article keeps a link to the source. Later, use **Re-sync from source** in the article's menu to pull in the newest version.

> [!NOTE]
> Never paste customer names, phone numbers or private details into an article. Articles are read by other agents and by the AI.

## Tips

- Keep one topic in one article. Short articles are found more easily.
- Use the words customers use, not internal names.
- Put the answer first, then the details.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting your article to be searchable straight away. It is a draft until it is published.
- Testing before you save. The test box only sees saved text.
- Uploading a large document with many topics. Split it into articles.
