Customers write in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese. You can translate an article into the other languages with AI, then read and fix the result. Every translation is an article of its own, linked to the original.

## Before you start

- The AI must be set up by your admin. If it is not, you will see a message like "AI is not set up, or the translate job is switched off". Ask your admin, or write the translation by hand.
- Admins can translate any article. Everyone else can translate only their own drafts. If you see "You can only translate your own drafts", ask an admin.
- Save the article first. A translation is made from the saved text, so the buttons wait while you have unsaved changes.

## Translate an article

1. Click **Knowledge** and open the article.
2. Save it if you have changed anything.
3. In the **Translations** card on the right, find the language you want. Click **Translate with AI**. To do every language at once, click **Translate to all**.
4. Confirm the question. It says the result is saved as a draft and that nothing is published.
5. Wait. The new draft opens when it is ready. It has a banner: AI translation of your article, review it before publishing.
6. Read every line. Check prices, dates, names and steps.
7. Fix what is wrong, then click **Save draft**.
8. An admin publishes it. Agents cannot publish, so the draft waits for a reviewer.

![The Translations card with one row for each language and the Translate with AI buttons.](/help/img/translations-01-card.png)

You can also translate from the library. In the **Translations** column, each language is a small chip. A chip with a plus sign starts a translation. A chip with a dot is one that exists already. The dot tells you if it is a draft, out of date, or still the AI text.

## Labels you may see

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI translation** | Nobody has edited the text yet. |
| **Edited by hand** | A person has changed the AI text. |
| **Draft** | It is not live yet. |
| **Out of date** | The original changed after this was translated. |

## When the original changes

Say you fix a price in the original. Its translations show **Out of date**, with the banner: The original changed after this was translated. You have three choices:

- Edit the translation by hand, then click **Mark up to date**.
- Click **Re-translate with AI**. This replaces the current text, including your edits, with a new draft. The app asks you to confirm first.
- Leave it. The translation stays as it is.

## What customers and AI see

When an article and its translation both match a question, only the one in the customer's language is used. A translation uses the original's files, unless you add files to the translation. Files you add replace the original's files for that language.

## Tips

- Translate only after the original is final.
- Ask a teammate who speaks the language to read it before it is published, if you can.
- Keep sentences short in the original. They translate better.

## Common mistakes

- Trusting the AI text without reading it.
- Re-translating after editing by hand. It replaces your edits.
- Deleting the original. It also deletes its translations, and the app warns you first.
