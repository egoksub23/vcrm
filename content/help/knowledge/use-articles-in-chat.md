You do not need to leave the Inbox to use the Knowledge base. You can search it beside the chat, and put an approved answer into your reply in one click.

## Before you start

Only **Published** articles are found. If an article is still a Draft, it will not appear. Insert never sends anything. It puts the text in your reply box, so you can edit it first.

## Three places to search

- The **Knowledge** tab in the row above the reply box (next to **Message**, **Comment** and **Snippets**). A panel slides up over the chat.
- The **Knowledge** tab in the right-hand column of the chat (next to **Contact**).
- The `/kb` command. Type `/kb` as the whole message, or `/kb refund` to search for refund.

## Insert an answer

1. Open a chat.
2. Open one of the three places above. With nothing typed, you see **Suggested for this chat**. These come from what the customer wrote last.
3. Or type in **Search the knowledge base…**.
4. Click **Show full** on a result to read all of it.
5. Click **Insert**. The text goes into your reply box, and you see a message: Added "Article title" to your reply.
6. Change the wording so it fits this customer. Check every price, date and name.
7. Send the reply as usual.

With `/kb`, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to insert the first match, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the list.

The text is formatted for the channel you are replying on. An email reply keeps the article's formatting.

## Files and pictures

Some articles have files or pictures. You see them as chips, and a hint says "These files are sent with the answer". After you insert the article, they appear above the reply box as chips that start with "From knowledge base:".

- The files are sent after your text.
- If the channel cannot carry files, a link to each file is added to the message instead.
- Click the cross on a chip to leave that file out of this reply.

## Draft with AI

Some results have **Draft with AI**. It asks the AI to write a reply from that one article only. It does not appear for **Agents only** articles. If AI is not set up, you see an error message and can write the reply yourself. After the draft appears, a line called **Based on** lists the articles it used, such as "1 · Refund policy". Click a chip to open the article. If files will go with the answer, you see them as **Will attach:** chips. Always read and edit an AI draft. See [AI assistance](/help/inbox/ai-assistance).

## Open the article or add a new one

- Click **Open** on a result to see the full article in a new tab. It shows only if your role can open the Knowledge page.
- Hover over a message in the chat and click **Add to knowledge base** to start a new article from that message. Use it on a good answer or a common question.
- In the Knowledge panel above the reply box, click **Add** to start an article from your draft reply.

An article you write is saved as a draft for an admin to publish. See [Write and edit articles](/help/knowledge/write-and-edit-articles).

## Tips

- Set the contact's **Language** in the Inbox right-hand column. Articles in that language rank first.
- If nothing is suggested, type a word the customer used. Try a short word first.
- The **Agents only** chip means the AI never uses that article. It is safe for you to use.

## Common mistakes

- Sending the inserted text without reading it.
- Forgetting that files will be sent with the answer.
- Searching for a draft article. Only published articles show up.
