The Knowledge base holds what your business already knows: prices, policies and how-to steps. You search it while you chat, and the AI assistant reads it when it drafts or sends replies.

## Open it

Click **Knowledge** in the sidebar. On the left you see:

- **All articles** and your **Collections**. A collection is a folder for one topic, such as Pricing. An article sits in one collection.
- **Views**: **Drafts**, **Review due**, **Agents only**, **Gaps** and **Insights**.

Above the list, four tiles show the number of Articles, how many are Published, how many times the AI used the knowledge base recently, and the Unanswered questions. Below them are a search box (**Search titles and collections…**), a **Language** filter and the table.

![The Knowledge base library with the left rail, the summary tiles and the article table.](/help/img/knowledge-overview-01-library.png)

In the table you see each article's Title, Collection, Translations, Audience, Status and how often the AI used it.

## Published or draft

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Agents can find it in a chat. The AI can use it if the audience allows. |
| **Draft** | Nobody can search it yet. It waits for a reviewer. |
| **Review due** | A published article whose "review by" date has arrived. Check it is still correct. |

Agents can write articles, but by default they are saved as drafts. An admin reviews and publishes them. Reviewers see them in **Settings → Approvals**, where approving an article publishes it.

## Who can use an article

Every article has an audience:

- **AI can use** means agents and the AI assistant can both find it.
- **Agents only** means only people can find it. The AI never reads it. Use this for internal notes, such as a discount limit.

You see the **Agents only** chip on these articles when you search from a chat.

## Languages

An article is written in English, Bahasa Melayu or Chinese. When you chat with a customer whose **Language** is set, articles in that language come first. See [Translations](/help/knowledge/translations).

## Unanswered and Insights

- **Unanswered** (the **Gaps** view) lists questions the AI could not answer because no article matched, with the most asked first. If you are allowed to write, each one has **Write article**, **Save agent reply**, **Dismiss** and **View chat**. Dismissed and resolved items can be reopened.
- **Insights** shows the articles the AI used most, the published articles it never used, and the articles that were written to fix a handoff.

## Search style

A small label at the top says either **Meaning search on** or **Keyword search**. With meaning search, "money back" finds a refund policy. With keyword search, the words must match. Your admin sets this up.

## What you can do by default

Everyone can read the library and search it from a chat. By default an Agent can add articles and Q&A pairs, and can edit or delete their own drafts. Publishing, collections and the **Reindex** button are for admins. If a button is greyed out, see [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Tips

- Search the library before you write a new article. A second article on the same topic confuses the AI and other agents.
- Read the **Review due** view now and then. Old prices cause wrong answers.
- Open **Unanswered** at the end of a shift. Each question is an article waiting to be written.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting for a draft to show up in chat search. Only published articles are found.
- Marking an article **Agents only** and then wondering why the AI never uses it.
