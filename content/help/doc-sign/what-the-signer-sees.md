The people you send a document to do not need a login or an account. This page describes what they see, so you can help them if they ask.

## The invitation

They get an email, or a WhatsApp message if you chose that for them. It has your message, the expiry date and a button with their private link. The link is for one person only: do not forward it. If someone needs to sign instead, [change the recipient](/help/doc-sign/remind-resend-and-cancel#change-the-recipient) so they get their own link.

The page works on a phone first, and on a tablet or a computer too.

## Step by step

1. **Code (only if you asked for one).** They ask for a 6-digit code, which is sent to them. It works for 10 minutes and allows five tries.
2. **Before you start.** A short statement says they agree to use electronic records and signatures. It also tells them that Halo records their name, email address, network address and device, the time, and what they do on the page. They can read the document first. They must tick **I agree** to continue. The wording and its version are saved with their signature. Your admin can change the wording. See [Settings and the WhatsApp template](/help/doc-sign/settings-and-whatsapp).
3. **Fill in.** They see the document with only their own fields marked. A bar shows how many required fields are done, and **Next field** jumps to the next unfinished one. Their answers are saved as they go, so they can close the page and come back to the same link until it expires.
4. **Sign.** They tap a signature field and choose to **draw** it with a finger or mouse, **type** their name, or **upload** a picture of their signature. They can reuse it for initials.
5. **Finish.** They tap **Finish**. The page confirms they have signed.

If the document is in signing order and it is not their turn, they see "It is not your turn yet" and nothing of the document.

## If they do not want to sign

They can choose **I do not want to sign** and give a reason. The sender is told and the document stops. Nobody else can sign it.

## After everyone has signed

The signed PDF is made, sealed and emailed to every signer and to you. The signer can also download it from their link, but only once the document is completed, not before. See [The audit trail and the sealed PDF](/help/doc-sign/audit-trail-and-sealed-pdf).

## Other messages the signer can see

| They see | Why |
|---|---|
| This link is not valid | The link was cut off when copied, or it was replaced by a newer one |
| This link has expired | The expiry date passed. Send it again |
| This document was cancelled | You cancelled it |
| It is not your turn yet | The document is in signing order |
| Please wait a moment | Too many tries came from their connection |

## Language

The page opens in the language you chose on the **Options** step, and the signer can change it on the page. English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese and Korean are available.

## Tips

- Tell people to expect the email, and to look in spam if it does not arrive.
- If they say the link does not work, check the address on the document's page and [resend](/help/doc-sign/remind-resend-and-cancel).

## Common mistakes

- **Passing a signer's link to someone else.** The link is for one person. To send a document to a different person, use **Change recipient** so that person gets their own link.
- **Expecting a signer to see more than the document.** A signer sees the document with what earlier signers completed on it, and nothing from any other document.
