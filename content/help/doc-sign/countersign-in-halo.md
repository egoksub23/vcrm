Some documents need a signature from someone on your own team, for example a director who countersigns after the merchant. When that person has a Halo login, they do not need to wait for an email or type a code. Halo knows who they are.

## Before you start

- You need permission to **countersign inside Halo**. Owners and admins have it. An admin can give it to another role in the role settings.
- The sender must have named you on the document as a **Halo user**, not as an outside email address. If you are not on the document by your Halo login, none of this appears for you.

## See what is waiting for you

1. Open **Secure Sign**. A number next to **Secure Sign** in the side menu counts the documents that wait for you.
2. On the **Documents** tab, click **Awaiting my signature**. The list shows the title, the reference, who sent it, when it was sent and when it expires.

A document only appears when it is your turn. If the document signs in order, it stays out of the list until the people before you have finished. When your turn comes you also get a notification in Halo.

## Sign

1. Click **Sign now** next to the document. You can also open the document and click **Sign now** at the top.
2. The signing page opens in the same tab. You are not asked for a verification code, because you are already signed in to Halo.
3. Agree to sign electronically, fill in your fields and finish, just like any other signer. See [What the signer sees](/help/doc-sign/what-the-signer-sees).

After you finish, the next person is invited, or the document is sealed when you were the last.

## What the record says

Every signing is kept in the document's history. For your turn it says that you were identified by your Halo sign-in, and the sealed certificate says the same in the language of the document. That is the honest record of how you were checked: by your login, not by a code sent to your email.

## Good to know

- **Sign now** makes a new link for you. The link in the email you may have received earlier stops working. Use the tab that Halo opened.
- Nobody else can open your turn. Not the sender, not an admin. **Sign now** works only for the person named on the document, and only when it is their turn.
- A person on the document who is a Halo user shows as **Halo user** in the **People** tab.

## Needs attention

Next to **Awaiting my signature**, the **Needs attention** shortcut lists what the sender should look at:

- documents that were **declined** or **expired** in the last 30 days;
- documents that could not be sealed;
- open documents where a message to someone **did not arrive**. Reminding the person, sending a new link or changing the recipient clears it once the message goes through.

Halo only knows about messages it could not hand over. If an email is accepted and bounces later, Halo is not told, so it cannot show it here. If someone says they never got an invitation, use **Resend** or **Change recipient** on the **People** tab. See [Remind, resend and cancel](/help/doc-sign/remind-resend-and-cancel).

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for the email.** You do not have to. Open **Awaiting my signature** and click **Sign now**.
- **Looking for a document that is not your turn yet.** With signing order on, it appears when the people before you have signed.
- **Using an old link.** After **Sign now**, only the newest link works.
- **Expecting a code.** A Halo user is not asked for one. If you are asked, you opened the old emailed link.
