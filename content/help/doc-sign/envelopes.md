An **envelope** is a group of two to six documents that go to the same people. Each person gets **one message and one link**, agrees to sign electronically **once**, and signs all their documents in one visit. This is useful for a set such as a contract, a fee schedule and a registration form.

Every document in an envelope is still a normal document: it has its own file, fields, signed PDF, certificate and history. The envelope only holds what is shared, the people and the options.

## Before you start

You need permission to send documents. An envelope holds **2 to 6 documents**, **300 pages** in all and **50 MB** in all. Each document counts toward your monthly limit of documents sent for signing, so an envelope of four documents uses four. If the workspace does not have room for all of them, the envelope is not sent and nothing is used.

## Make an envelope

1. Click **New document**, then **Send several documents as one envelope**.
2. Choose the documents: pick templates, and add one file if you need to. Use the arrows to put them in the order the people will see them.
3. Give the envelope a title and, if you like, a contact, a ticket or a deal. Click **Create the envelope**.
4. On the envelope page, click **Prepare** on each document to place its fields, exactly as for a document on its own.
5. In **People**, add each person **once** and say what they are on each document. Roles with the same name on different documents line up by themselves. A person can be on some documents and not others.
6. In **Options**, set the signing order, the expiry, the reminders and whether a code is needed. These apply to every document of the envelope.
7. Open **Review and send**. It lists, document by document, what still needs fixing. When it is clear, click **Send**.

All the documents are sent together or not at all. If one of them is not ready, nothing is sent and the review tells you which one.

## Signing order

If you switch on signing order, each person has a **step**. The next step is invited only when everyone in the step before has finished **all** the documents, and they are invited once, with one message each. See [Signing order](/help/doc-sign/signing-order).

## What the signer sees

The signer opens one link. They see the list of documents and their own state on each one. They agree once, then go through the documents one after another. They can open the other documents in any order, and nothing is final until they finish the last one. When they finish, every document they have left is signed in turn.

If a document needs something fixed, the page takes them to that document and shows what is missing; the ones already done stay done.

When everyone has signed, each person gets **one email** with all the signed documents attached (or a link to download them if they are large). Each document is sealed on its own, with its own certificate. The certificate also names the envelope and says how many documents it held.

## Follow an envelope

- In the **Documents** list an envelope is **one row**, marked with the number of documents. Its status is worked out from its documents. Click it to open the envelope page.
- The envelope page shows each document with its state, each person with their state on every document, and the actions below.
- A document that is part of an envelope shows a box at the top with the other documents and a link to the envelope. The actions that change people or timing are done on the envelope, not on one document.
- Someone who opens the public check page of a signed document sees that it was signed in an envelope.

## Remind, resend, change and cancel

- **Remind** and **Resend** work per person. A resend gives them one new link, and the old link stops working.
- **Change recipient** replaces a person on all their documents at once. It is refused once that person has signed any of them.
- **Give more time** sets one new expiry date for every document that is still open.
- **Cancel the envelope** stops every document and tells the waiting people once. You can cancel only while no document has been signed by everyone yet. After that the envelope can no longer be cancelled.
- If a person **declines**, every document that is not yet fully signed is declined, and the envelope stops.
- You can delete an envelope that is still a draft. One that was sent is cancelled instead, and a signed one is kept for its retention period. See [Retention](/help/doc-sign/retention).

> [!NOTE]
> A document of an envelope cannot be forwarded to someone else, and a test document cannot be put in an envelope. An envelope cannot be made from the automation builder or the public API yet; you make it here.

## Common mistakes

- **Adding the same person twice, once for each document.** Add them once and give them a role on each document. The same email address twice is refused.
- **Leaving a person on no document.** Everyone on the list must be on at least one document, or be removed.
- **Giving one role to two people.** A role on a document goes to one person. If two people must sign, the document needs two roles; set that up in the template.
- **Expecting to change the people after sending.** Once sent, use **Change recipient** for someone who has not signed anything. You cannot add a new person to a sent envelope.
- **Trying to cancel one document.** Cancel the whole envelope. A single document of an envelope cannot be cancelled or reminded on its own.
- **Choosing too many or too large documents.** Two to six documents, 300 pages and 50 MB is the limit. Split a bigger set into two envelopes.
