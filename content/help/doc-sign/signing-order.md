By default everyone on a document is invited at once and anyone can sign first. If your document needs people to sign one after another, tick **This document needs signing order**.

## Choose the order

On the **People** step of a document:

1. Add the people.
2. Tick **This document needs signing order**.
3. Drag the people, or use the arrows, until the numbers are in the order you want.

With the box ticked, each person has a number. With it off, no numbers are shown and the order does not matter.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The signing order cannot be changed after the document is sent.

## What happens when it is sent

- **Order off.** Everyone is invited at once.
- **Order on.** Only person number 1 is invited. When that person signs, the next one is invited automatically, and so on. The last signature finishes the document.

A person who is not invited yet can already have a link, but it only shows "It is not your turn yet". They see nothing of the document and are messaged when it is their turn.

## Reminders and stops

- Reminders go only to the person whose turn it is.
- A **decline**, a **cancel** or an **expiry** stops the whole chain. Nobody after that can sign.
- On the document's page, a person who is not invited yet shows "Not invited yet".

## Fillers and signers

A **filler** completes fields but does not sign. In an ordered document a filler takes a place in the order like anyone else.

## Set it for every document of a kind

A category or a template can tick the box for you. You can still change it on each document before you send.

## Tips

- Use order when the second person must see what the first one entered, for example a director who signs after the merchant.
- Leave it off when the order does not matter. It is faster, because nobody waits.

## Common mistakes

- **Putting the same person at two numbers.** With signing order on, each signer must be a different person, so the same email address twice is refused.
