By default everyone on a document is invited at once and anyone can sign first. If your document needs people to sign one after another, tick **This document needs signing order**.

## Choose the order

On the **People** step of a document:

1. Add the people.
2. Tick **This document needs signing order**.
3. Drag the people, or use the arrows, or type a **step number** for each person, until the steps are in the order you want.

With the box ticked, each person has a step number. With it off, no numbers are shown and the order does not matter.

**People who share a number sign together.** Give two people the same step number and they form one step: both are invited when the step begins, and the next step begins only when both have signed. The page shows them under a heading such as **Step 1 (2 people)**. See [Forwarding and signing together](/help/doc-sign/forwarding-and-parallel-signing).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The signing order cannot be changed after the document is sent.

## What happens when it is sent

- **Order off.** Everyone is invited at once.
- **Order on.** Only the people in step 1 are invited. When all of them have signed, the people in the next step are invited automatically, and so on. The last signature finishes the document.

A person whose step has not begun has no link yet. They are messaged, with a link, when their step begins. The history says why: "invited because Ali finished", or "invited because the previous step finished" when a step had several people.

## Reminders and stops

- Reminders go only to the people whose step it is.
- A **decline**, a **cancel** or an **expiry** stops the whole chain. Nobody after that can sign.
- On the document's page, a person who is not invited yet shows "Not invited yet". Until their step begins you can still correct their name or email with **Change recipient**, or move them to a later step with **Move to**.

## Fillers and signers

A **filler** completes fields but does not sign. In an ordered document a filler takes a place in a step like anyone else.

## Set it for every document of a kind

A category or a template can tick the box for you. You can still change it on each document before you send.

## Tips

- Use order when the second person must see what the first one entered, for example a director who signs after the merchant.
- Leave it off when the order does not matter. It is faster, because nobody waits.

## Common mistakes

- **Putting the same person in two steps.** With signing order on, each signer must be a different person, so the same email address twice is refused, even in the same step.
- **Giving two people one number by accident.** The same number means they sign at the same time. Check the headings on the People step before you send.
