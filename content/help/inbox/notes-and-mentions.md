Sometimes you need to tell your team something about a chat without telling the customer. Vircle CRM has two ways: **internal comments** in the chat, and **session notes** about the customer.

## Before you start

You need to be allowed to add internal notes. If not, the **Comment** tab is greyed out. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Write an internal comment

1. Open the chat.
2. Press the **Comment** tab (it has a lock icon) above the reply box. The box turns amber.
3. Type your comment. The box says "Add an internal comment — teammates only, never sent to the customer".
4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or the amber send button.

![The reply box in Comment mode, with an amber border and the hint that comments are for the team only](/help/img/notes-and-mentions-01-comment-mode.png)

Your comment appears in the chat as a wide amber card with a lock and the words "Internal comment", your name and the time. Comments can be up to 4,000 characters. They work even when the 24-hour WhatsApp timer has expired.

Press the **Message** tab to go back to writing to the customer.

> [!WARNING]
> Check the amber colour before you press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Text typed in the **Message** tab goes to the customer. Once you post a comment, there is no button to edit or delete it in the chat.

## Mention a teammate

A mention sends the person an alert, so they see your comment quickly.

1. In **Comment** mode, type <kbd>@</kbd> and the first letters of their name. Or press the **Mention someone** button (the @ icon).
2. Click their name in the list, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Tab</kbd> to pick the first name.
3. Finish your comment and send it.

The person gets a **You were mentioned** alert in **Notifications**. It names you and the customer. Clicking it opens the chat.

Two things to know:

- Only a name you pick from the list counts as a mention. If you type "@Aisha" and ignore the list, or edit the name afterwards, Aisha may not be alerted.
- You do not get an alert for mentioning yourself.

## Session notes

A **session note** is a note about the customer, not about one message. It stays with the customer, so you see it in every chat with them.

On a wide screen, session notes are in the column between the chat and the customer details, under **Ticket history**. Open the column with the ticket button in the chat header.

1. Click the box **Add a session note…**.
2. Type your note.
3. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or the plus button (**Add note**).

Each note shows who wrote it and when. Long notes are folded. Press **Show more** to read all. To change your own note, press the pencil (**Edit note**) and then **Save**. To remove it, press the bin (**Delete note**) and confirm. Admins can change or delete anyone's notes.

Writing session notes needs permission to edit contacts.

> [!NOTE]
> The session-notes column only shows on wide screens. On a small screen, open the customer in the Contacts page to read their notes.

## What the customer can never see

These are for your team only:

- Internal comments and hand-over notes.
- The closing note and the "Closed by" and "Reopened by" lines.
- The **Session log**.
- Session notes.
- The "AI answered from" note that lists which articles the AI used.

Everything in the Message tab, and anything you send with **Send template**, does reach the customer.

## Tips

- Start a comment with what you need, for example "@Devi please check the refund limit before I reply."
- Use a comment to explain a decision, for example why you did not refund.
- Put facts about the customer that stay true (a preferred name, a special arrangement) in a session note.

## Common mistakes

- **Typing a comment in the Message tab.** It goes to the customer. Look for the amber colour first.
- **Typing @Name without picking from the list.** The teammate may not get an alert.
- **Leaving lasting facts only in a comment.** Comments move up the chat as new messages arrive. Put facts that stay true in a session note.
