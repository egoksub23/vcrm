Closing a chat tells your team the customer is sorted. Every close needs a short note, so anyone who looks at the chat later knows how it ended.

## Before you start

You need to be allowed to manage conversations. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Close a chat

1. Open the chat.
2. Press the **Status** button in the chat header. It shows the current status: Open, Pending or Closed.
3. Choose **Closed**. A box titled **Close conversation** opens.
4. Write a note, for example "Refund processed, customer confirmed receipt." The note can be up to 1,000 characters.
5. Press **Close**.

The **Close** button stays greyed out until the note has some text. Nothing is sent to the customer when you close a chat.

![The Close conversation box with a note field and Cancel and Close buttons](/help/img/close-and-reopen-01-close-dialog.png)

### If your workspace uses AI

When AI is set up, the box first says "Drafting a note from the chat…". Then it fills in a draft note and says "Drafted by AI. Edit before closing." You can:

- Edit the draft, or replace it.
- Press **Draft again** to get a new one. This replaces what is in the box.
- Keep or untick the box "Also add the label ..." if the AI suggests one of your existing labels. It is applied after the chat closes.

If the box says "The AI budget for this month is used up. Write the note yourself." or "Could not draft a note. Write it yourself.", type the note yourself. The draft is only a suggestion. The chat closes only when you press **Close**.

## What you see after closing

- The chat status changes to **Closed**. The status dot in the list changes colour (grey, unless your admin picked other colours).
- A wide amber line appears in the chat: "Closed by" and your name, with the time. Under it is your note, marked "Closure note", and the words "Only visible to your team".
- The **Session log** in the right-hand column records it too.

Your admin may also have rules that run when a chat closes. These are set up by admins.

## The session log

The **Session log** is at the bottom of the **Contact** tab in the right-hand column. It lists what happened to the chat, oldest first, with who did it and when:

- Assigned to a person, or unassigned.
- Assigned to a team, or removed from a team.
- Priority changed from one level to another.
- Closed (with the note).
- Reopened.

Point at a time such as "2 hours ago" to see the exact date and time.

## Reopen a chat

1. Open the closed chat. Use the **Closed** filter to find it.
2. Press the **Status** button.
3. Choose **Open** or **Pending**.

A reopen does not ask for a note. The amber line says "Reopened by" and your name.

## When the customer writes again

If a customer sends a new message to a closed chat, the chat reopens by itself and becomes **Open**. The amber line says "Reopened — customer wrote again". The same words appear in the **Session log**. This means a closed chat can appear again in your **Open** list and in the unread count.

## Tips

- Write the note for a colleague who has never seen the chat. One or two clear sentences are enough.
- To close many finished chats at once, see [Views and bulk actions](/help/inbox/views-and-bulk-actions). The bulk close uses one shared note for all of them, so make it general.

## Common mistakes

- **Writing a closing note that only makes sense to you.** Say what was done and what the customer agreed.
- **Closing a chat that still needs an answer.** Leave it **Open**, or set it to **Pending**, so it does not look finished.
- **Being surprised when a closed chat comes back.** A new customer message reopens it automatically.
