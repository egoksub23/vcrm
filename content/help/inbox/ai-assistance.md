AI can save you typing, but it is a helper, not the decision maker. You read every AI draft before it goes to a customer.

## Before you start

- Your admin has to set up AI. If they have not, AI buttons show a message such as "AI isn't set up yet". Ask your admin.
- You need permission to use AI. If not, the AI buttons are greyed out with "Read-only — your role can't use AI". See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
- AI uses a monthly budget set by your admin. When it is used up, AI features tell you so, and you write things yourself.

## Draft a reply

1. Open the chat.
2. Press the sparkle button (**Draft a reply with AI**) beside the reply box.
3. Wait a moment. A spinner shows on the button.
4. The draft appears in the reply box. Edit it.
5. Press **Send** when you are happy with it.

Nothing is sent until you press **Send**. A hint under the box says: "Tap the ✨ to draft a reply with AI — you can edit it before sending".

The AI uses the chat and your team's Knowledge articles. It writes in the customer's language if you set **Language** in the **Contact fields** of the right-hand column.

### The "Based on" chips

If the AI used articles, chips appear above the reply box: "Based on" and the number and title of each article. Click a chip to open the article in a new tab (if your role can open Knowledge). Use them to check where an answer came from.

Some articles have files, such as a price list. These show as "Will attach:" chips and are sent after your text. Press the cross on a chip to leave a file out. If the channel cannot carry files, a link to each file is added to your message instead.

### Draft from one article

Open the **Knowledge** tab in the right-hand column (or the **Knowledge** tab above the reply box), find an article, and press **Draft with AI**. The AI then writes a reply from that article only. See [Use articles in chat](/help/knowledge/use-articles-in-chat).

### If the draft fails

You may see "Couldn't draft a reply.", "The assistant didn't return a reply." or "Couldn't reach the AI assistant." Try again, or write the reply yourself.

## Draft a closing note

When you close a chat, the box may fill in a draft note and suggest a label. You edit it and press **Close**. See [Close and reopen](/help/inbox/close-and-reopen).

## Summarise a conversation

Use this when you take over a long chat.

1. Open the chat.
2. In the **Contact** tab of the right-hand column, press **Summarise this conversation**.
3. Read the summary that appears under **Summary**.

The summary covers who the customer is, what they want, what has been promised and what is still open. It is not saved. You can press **Summarise again**, **Copy** or **Hide** on the card. It always carries a reminder: "Written by AI from the recent messages. Check it before relying on it."

If AI is not set up, you see "AI is not set up. An admin can do that in AI Agents → Setup."

## When AI answers customers by itself

Your workspace may use an AI auto-reply. Then:

- Messages the AI sent carry a small **AI** badge. Point at it to see "Sent automatically by the AI assistant".
- A bar above the reply box says "AI assistant is replying automatically" for chats nobody owns. Press **Take over** to stop the AI and make the chat yours. See [Assign and hand over](/help/inbox/assign-and-transfer).
- After an auto-reply, the chat gets a private note starting with **AI answered from** and the article names. Only your team sees this note. Click an article name to open it, if your role can open Knowledge.
- If the AI handed the chat over, the bar "AI assistant is paused here" may show a short note it left for you. **Resume AI** hands the chat back to the AI.

## When to trust AI, and when to edit

Always check:

- **Facts and numbers**: prices, dates, refund amounts, opening hours.
- **Promises**: the AI does not know what you are allowed to promise.
- **Names and details** that belong to this customer.
- **Tone**: shorten it, and make it sound like you.
- **Language**: it should match the customer's.

It is usually safe to use a draft for a simple, common question that your Knowledge article answers clearly. Take extra care with complaints, money, legal or safety topics, and anything the article does not cover. If the "Based on" chips show no articles, the draft did not come from your team's answers, so check it more carefully.

## Tips

- Improve answers at the source. If an article is wrong or missing, tell whoever manages Knowledge. See [Use articles in chat](/help/knowledge/use-articles-in-chat).
- Use **Summarise this conversation** before you reply to a chat someone else worked on.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending a draft without reading it.**
- **Trusting a summary as a record.** It is not saved and can miss details. Read the last messages too.
- **Fixing one wrong AI answer only in the chat.** The next customer may get the same mistake. Report the article.
