The Inbox is where you read and answer customers. This page shows what is on the screen. Later pages show how to do each task.

## The layout

On a computer the Inbox has three main areas, from left to right:

1. **The conversation list.** Every chat, email and comment thread.
2. **The open chat.** The messages, and the reply box at the bottom.
3. **The right-hand column.** Details about the customer.

![The Inbox with the conversation list on the left, an open chat in the middle and the customer details on the right](/help/img/inbox-overview-01-layout.png)

On a wide screen there is one more column between the chat and the customer details. It holds **Ticket history** and **Session notes** for this customer. Open or close it with the ticket button in the chat header.

On a phone or small tablet you see one panel at a time. Tap a chat to open it, and use the back arrow at the top left to return to the list. The right-hand columns are not shown on small screens.

## The three tabs

At the top of the list are three tabs. Each has a number.

| Tab | What is in it | What the number means |
|---|---|---|
| **Chats** | WhatsApp, Web Widget, Messenger and Instagram messages | Chats with unread messages |
| **Emails** | Email and Gmail | Email conversations with unread messages |
| **Comments** | Public comments on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts | Comments still waiting for a first response |

The number counts conversations, not single messages. Comments have their own page: see [Social comments](/help/inbox/social-comments).

## Reading the list

Each row is one customer. From left to right you can see:

- The customer's name (or phone number), the channel icon, and a flag if the priority is not Normal.
- How long ago the last message arrived.
- The start of the last message.
- A **number bubble** when there are unread messages. Opening the chat clears it.
- A **clock chip** when the customer is waiting for your reply. It starts amber and turns red when the wait passes the reply target your admin set. Point at it to see when the customer wrote.
- A small **team dot** and a **status dot** (Open, Pending or Closed). Point at a dot to see its name.
- Coloured **tags and labels**. Up to three show, then "+2" and so on. See [Tags, labels and priority](/help/inbox/tags-labels-priority).

The list updates on its own, and a conversation jumps to the top of the list the moment a new message arrives on it — you do not need to refresh to see it move. If you think something is missing, press the refresh button in the chat header.

## Search and filters

Type in **Search conversations...** to search by customer name, phone number, or the last message shown in the list.

Under the search box are the filters:

![The top of the conversation list: the three tabs, the search box and the row of filter buttons](/help/img/inbox-overview-02-filters.png)

- The ownership tabs: **All**, **Mine** and **Unassigned** are one click each. **Others** opens a drop-down for **Unread**, **Open**, **Pending** and **Closed**.
- **Tags**, **Labels**, **Company** and **Team** drop-downs. Each only appears when your workspace has something to choose.
- A channel drop-down, such as **All chat channels**. You can tick more than one channel.
- The flag button filters by priority.
- Four small icon buttons: **Views**, **Toggle sort order** (Recent or Priority), **Select** and the bin (**Pending Delete**).

For saved views and bulk actions, see [Views and bulk actions](/help/inbox/views-and-bulk-actions).

## The open chat

Above the messages is a header with the customer's name, tags and labels, and, on WhatsApp chats, a **timer** (hidden on very narrow screens). The timer counts down 24 hours from the customer's last WhatsApp message. When it says **Expired**, the reply box is locked while WhatsApp is the selected channel. Email, web chat, Messenger and Instagram have no 24-hour limit. See [Reply to customers](/help/inbox/reply-to-customers).

The buttons on the right of the header are:

- **Ticket** and **panel** toggles for the side columns.
- **Refresh**.
- The channel name.
- **Raise Ticket**.
- **Priority**, **Status**, **Assign**, **Team** and **Labels**.

Messages from the customer are on the left. Yours are on the right. Internal comments are wide amber cards that the customer never sees.

## The right-hand column

The column has two tabs: **Contact** and **Knowledge**.

![The right-hand column on the Contact tab, showing summary, contact fields, tags, labels, deals and session log](/help/img/inbox-overview-03-right-column.png)

On the **Contact** tab, top to bottom:

- **Summarise this conversation**: an AI summary on request. See [AI assistance](/help/inbox/ai-assistance).
- **Contact fields**: name, phone number, email address, company, country and language. Click a value to change it.
- **Tags** and **Conversation labels**.
- **Deals** linked to this customer.
- **Session log**: who assigned, closed or reopened the chat, and when.

The **Knowledge** tab searches your team's articles. See [Use articles in chat](/help/knowledge/use-articles-in-chat).

## Tips

- Use **Unread** to find what you have not seen, and **Mine** to see your own chats.
- The panel button in the chat header hides the right-hand column when you need more room. Vircle CRM remembers your choice.

## Common mistakes

- **Searching for an email address or a tag.** Search only looks at name, phone number and last message. Use the filters for tags.
- **Looking for an email in Chats.** Emails are in the **Emails** tab.
- **Thinking a filter is missing.** A filter only shows when it has something to choose. For example, **Team** appears only when your admin has created teams.
