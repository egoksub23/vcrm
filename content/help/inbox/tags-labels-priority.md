Tags, labels and priority help you sort and find chats. They are easy to mix up, so this page explains each one.

## Tags and labels are different things

| | Contact **tags** | Conversation **labels** |
|---|---|---|
| About | The person, for example "VIP" | This one chat, for example "Refund" |
| Where it lives | On the contact. It follows the customer into every chat and into Contacts | On the conversation only |
| How it looks | An outlined pill with a small dot | A filled, tinted pill |

Each tag and label has its own colour. The same colour is used in the conversation list, the chat header and the right-hand column, so you can scan the list for what you need.

![The Tags and Conversation labels sections in the right-hand column, showing coloured chips and the plus button](/help/img/tags-labels-priority-01-tags-and-labels.png)

## Add a tag to a customer

1. Open the chat.
2. In the right-hand column, find **Tags** and press the small plus button (**Add tag**).
3. Type in **Search tags** to narrow the list, then click a tag to add it. A tick shows the ones already added.
4. Click outside the list when you are done. You can pick several before you close it.

To remove a tag, press the small cross on its chip.

If the list says "No tags exist yet", your admin has not created any. You need permission to edit contacts to add or remove tags.

## Add a label to a chat

You can do it in two places:

- In the chat header, press **Labels** and tick the labels you want. This button only appears when your workspace has labels. A number on it shows how many the chat has.
- In the right-hand column, under **Conversation labels**, press the plus button (**Add label**).

To remove a label, untick it, or press the cross on its chip.

You need permission to manage conversations. If the list says "No labels exist yet", your admin has not created any.

## Need a new tag or label?

You cannot create them in the Inbox. Ask your admin. By default, agents can also suggest new tags and labels in **Settings**, and a reviewer approves them before they can be used. See [Approvals](/help/working-together/approvals).

## Set the priority

Every chat has a priority: **Urgent**, **High**, **Normal** or **Low**. New chats start at **Normal**.

1. Open the chat.
2. Press the flag button in the chat header. It shows the current priority.
3. Choose a new one.

![The Priority menu open with Urgent, High, Normal and Low](/help/img/tags-labels-priority-02-priority-menu.png)

A small flag appears in the conversation list for any chat that is not Normal. The change is recorded in the **Session log**. Automations set up by your admin can also change priority.

To see the most important chats first, press the two-arrows button (**Toggle sort order**) in the filter row above the list. It changes colour while priority sorting is on. Chats are then sorted Urgent, High, Normal, Low, and by newest first inside each level. Press it again to go back to sorting by **Recent** activity. To show one level only, use the flag button under the search box.

## What the colours mean

| Colour is on | What it shows |
|---|---|
| The **status dot** in the list | Open, Pending or Closed. |
| The **priority flag** | Urgent, High or Low. Normal chats have no flag. |
| The **clock chip** | Amber: the customer is waiting. Red: they have waited longer than your reply target. |
| Tags and labels | Whatever colour the tag or label was given. |

Your admin can change the status and priority colours, so yours may differ from another workspace.

## Tips

- Use a label for the topic (Billing, Refund) and a tag for who the customer is (VIP, Merchant).
- To find all chats with a topic, use **Labels** above the list. If you tick two labels, you see chats with either one.

## Common mistakes

- **Adding a tag when you meant a label.** A tag stays on the customer until someone removes it, so it shows on their next chat too.
- **Searching for a tag in the search box.** Search does not look at tags. Use the **Tags** filter.
- **Using Urgent for everything.** Then nothing stands out.
