Every chat can have one owner (a person) and one team. This page shows how to take a chat, give it to someone else, and make sure they know.

## Before you start

You need to be allowed to manage conversations. If not, the **Assign**, **Team**, **Status** and **Priority** buttons are greyed out. Point at one to see "Read-only — your role can't manage conversations". See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Find your chats and the queue

Use the ownership tabs above the conversation list.

- **Mine** shows the chats assigned to you.
- **Unassigned** shows chats that have no person assigned **and** are still Open. A chat that is Pending or Closed and has no owner does not appear here.

Your admin may also have rules that assign new chats automatically.

## Opening an unassigned chat claims it

If a chat in **Unassigned** has nobody assigned, simply opening it assigns it to you — the first agent to open it gets it. This stops two people from working the same chat by accident. If you open a chat and see you are suddenly its owner, that is why; use **Unassign** if you opened it by mistake.

## Take a chat yourself

1. Open the chat.
2. Press the **Assign** button in the chat header. If someone owns the chat, the button shows their name instead.
3. Choose your own name. It has "(me)" after it.
4. Press **Assign** in the box that opens.

## Hand a chat to a teammate

1. Open the chat and press the **Assign** button (or the current owner's name).
2. Choose a teammate. A small dot beside each name shows if they are online, away or offline. Point at the dot for details.
3. A box titled "Assign to" and their name opens. Type a short note for the new owner. It is optional.
4. Press **Assign**.

![The Assign menu open, listing teammates with online dots](/help/img/assign-and-transfer-01-assign-menu.png)

The note is saved as an internal comment that starts with "Handoff note:". It sits in the chat just before the assignment, so the new owner sees it first. The customer never sees it.

![The box for a hand-over note, with a text area, Cancel and Assign buttons](/help/img/assign-and-transfer-02-handoff-dialog.png)

A good note says what the customer wants, what you already did, and what is left. For example: "Customer confirmed refund eligibility. Just needs the payment processed."

## Give up a chat

Open the **Assign** menu and choose **Unassign**. This option only appears when the chat has an owner. There is no note box for this.

## Route a chat to a team

A team is a group of people, such as Payments or Tech. It is separate from the person who owns the chat.

1. Press the **Team** button in the chat header.
2. Choose a team.

To remove the team, open the same menu and choose **Remove from team**. A coloured dot in the conversation list shows which team owns a chat. You can filter the list by team.

## Who gets notified

- The person you assign gets an alert in **Notifications** that says you assigned them a conversation with the customer's name. The **Notifications** item in their sidebar shows a number until they read it. (Desktop alerts are only for new customer messages, not for assignments.)
- If you assign a chat to **yourself**, no alert is sent.
- **Unassign** and **team** changes do not send an alert by themselves.
- The hand-over note does not send its own alert. If it is urgent, also write an internal comment that mentions them with @. See [Notes and mentions](/help/inbox/notes-and-mentions).

The right-hand column has a **Session log** that records each assignment, team change and priority change, and who made it.

## When AI is answering

If your workspace uses the AI auto-reply and nobody owns the chat, a bar above the reply box says "AI assistant is replying automatically". Press **Take over** to pause the AI and make the chat yours. To give it back, press **Resume AI** on the bar that says "AI assistant is paused here". Resuming removes you as the owner. See [AI assistance](/help/inbox/ai-assistance).

## Tips

- Check a teammate's dot before you assign. Offline people may not see the alert for a while.
- To move many chats at once, use the bulk **Assign** button. See [Views and bulk actions](/help/inbox/views-and-bulk-actions). Bulk assign has no note box.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for a chat in Unassigned after it went to Pending.** Only chats that are Open and have no owner appear there.
- **Being surprised you now own a chat you only opened to look at.** Opening an unassigned chat claims it automatically. Use **Unassign** to give it back.
- **Writing the hand-over note in the reply box.** That would go to the customer. Use the note box that opens after you pick a teammate, or the **Comment** tab.
- **Assigning to a team and thinking a person will be told.** Assigning to a team does not alert anyone.
