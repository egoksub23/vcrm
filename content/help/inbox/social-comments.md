The **Comments** tab collects public comments on your Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts, including ads. It sits next to **Chats** and **Emails** at the top of the conversation list.

Comments are not chats. Each one is a public comment under a post, so anyone who sees the post can see your reply.

> [!NOTE]
> Comments only appear once your admin has connected those channels and allowed comments. If the list says "Comments on your Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts appear here once those channels are connected", ask your admin.

## Before you start

You need permission to moderate public comments to reply, hide, or mark comments. Without it you can read comments, but the reply tools, the tick boxes and the refresh button do not appear. Deleting comments needs a separate permission that admins have by default. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Read the list

![The Comments tab showing the To do view, source icons, and one row per post with its picture, caption, how many comments are waiting and the latest comment](/help/img/social-comments-01-list.png)

The list has **one row per post**, not one row per comment. A post with fifteen comments is still one row, so one busy post can no longer bury everything else.

Each row shows:

- The post's picture with the Facebook, Instagram or TikTok icon, then the post's caption.
- A badge such as **3 to do**: how many of its comments still wait for a first response. It is hidden when nothing is waiting.
- How many comments the post has, and the **latest comment** with who wrote it and how long ago.
- A blue dot and a bold caption when the post has comments that arrived since you last opened it. The dot is yours alone: opening a post clears it for you, not for your teammates.

A new comment on a post never adds a second row. The post's row moves to the top, its numbers go up and its dot lights.

Above the list:

- The number on the **Comments** tab is how many comments (not posts) still wait for a first response.
- Use the pills to change the view. They filter posts. **To do** shows posts with at least one comment waiting for a first response. **Handled** shows posts with nothing waiting and at least one comment replied to or resolved. **Spam** shows posts with at least one comment marked as spam, so a post can be in **To do** and **Spam** at the same time. **All** shows every post that has comments. The line under the pills repeats the rule for the pill you chose.
- Use **Search comments…** to find a post by its caption, or by the text, the name or the username of any of its comments.
- Click **All** or the Facebook, Instagram or TikTok icon to show one source.
- Switch on **Flat list** to see one row per comment, the way the list used to work, with the single comment opened on the right. The choice is remembered on this device for your login.
- Small tags show more: **Ad post** and **Sample** (a test comment your admin added to try the screens).
- Press **Load more** at the bottom for more posts.

New Facebook and Instagram comments arrive within seconds. TikTok comments can take a few minutes. To check TikTok now, press the refresh button beside the search box. It says how many new comments it found.

## Open a post

Click a post. On a phone the thread fills the screen and the arrow at the top takes you back to the list.

On the right you see the post (**On this post**, its picture, caption and **View post**) and under it **every comment on that post, oldest first, like a conversation**. It opens scrolled to the newest comment.

- A reply sits indented under the comment it answers.
- Your own replies, and comments from your page, show in colour on the right as **You**.
- When new comments arrived since you last looked, a **New** line marks where they start. A comment that arrives while the post is open appears by itself, under that line.
- A tag on a comment shows its state: **Replied**, **Resolved**, **Spam**, **Hidden**, **Deleted** or **Sample**.

![One post open with the post card, the comments as a conversation, the Reply and Private reply buttons under each comment, and the reply box at the bottom](/help/img/social-comments-02-detail.png)

## Reply to a comment

1. Press **Reply** under the comment you want to answer. The reply box at the bottom says "Replying to", the author and the start of their comment.
2. Type in the box. A counter shows the limit.
3. Press **Send**, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

To answer a different comment, press **Reply** under that one. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or **Cancel (Esc)**, to clear the target without sending.

The reply is public: everyone who sees the post can see it. The limits are 8,000 characters on Facebook, 2,200 on Instagram and 1,200 on TikTok. After you reply, that comment moves to **Handled** and shows **Replied**. A post leaves **To do** once none of its comments is waiting.

## Send a private message

Press **Private reply** under a comment instead. The reply box then says "Private message to", the author, and "Write a private message. Only this person will see it."

- You can send **one** private message per comment, within 7 days of the comment.
- After that, the conversation only continues if the person replies.
- TikTok does not allow private messages to people who comment.

When it is not available, the button is greyed out. Hover over it to read why.

## When one person comments many times

When one person has **three or more comments still waiting** on a post, their comments fold into a single line such as **ayakorose645 · 11 comments**.

- Click the line to read each comment. Every comment inside has its own buttons.
- **Mark all handled**, **Hide all** and **Spam all** act on all of that person's waiting comments at once. **Hide all** and **Spam all** ask you to confirm first.
- If some comments cannot be changed, for example because the platform refuses one, a red box under the thread lists each of them with the reason. Nothing is skipped silently.
- The line dissolves by itself once fewer than three of their comments are waiting.

## Act on several comments

Tick the box beside two or more comments. A bar appears above the reply box saying how many are selected, with **Mark handled**, **Spam** and **Hide**. **Spam** and **Hide** ask you to confirm. **Clear selection** unticks everything. Failures are listed the same way as for a person's comments.

## Hide or unhide a comment

Press **Hide** under a comment to remove it from public view without deleting it. It shows the **Hidden** tag. Press **Unhide** to bring it back.

## Delete a comment

**Delete** is only shown to people allowed to delete comments, which is admins by default. A box asks: "Delete this comment on the platform? This cannot be undone." On TikTok you can only delete your own comments. Hide other people's comments instead. Delete is not part of the bulk actions.

## Mark a comment handled

Under each customer comment:

- **Mark handled** when nothing more is needed.
- **Spam** to move it to **Spam** without replying.
- **Reopen** to put a handled or spam comment back in **To do**.

## Why an action can be unavailable

When you cannot do something, the button is greyed out and the reason shows when you hover over it, or in the reply box. The common ones:

- "This comment was deleted, so there is nothing more to do."
- "This is a comment from you or your page."
- "Instagram only lets you reply to a top-level comment. Reply to the first comment in the thread instead."
- "Instagram does not allow replies to a hidden comment. Unhide it first."
- "TikTok has no way to message someone who commented. Reply publicly instead."
- "A private message was already sent for this comment. They can continue the conversation by replying to it."
- "Private messages can only be sent within 7 days of the comment."
- "We do not know who wrote this comment, so we cannot message them."

In **Flat list** mode a comment also lists **Recent actions**, such as "Replied · done", so you can see what has already been done and whether it worked.

## Tips

- If the comment mentions a problem you cannot solve in public, reply briefly in public, then use a private message to ask for details.
- Do not post personal details in a public reply.
- In **Flat list** mode, if the customer is already a contact, the header shows "Contact:" and their name.

## Common mistakes

- **Replying in public with private information.** Anyone can read it.
- **Trying to send a second private message.** Only one is allowed per comment.
- **Marking spam by accident.** Use **Reopen** to undo it.
- **Hiding someone's comments by accident.** **Hide all** asks first, and **Unhide** under each comment brings them back.
