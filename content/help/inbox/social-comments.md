The **Comments** tab collects public comments on your Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts, including ads. It sits next to **Chats** and **Emails** at the top of the conversation list.

Comments are not chats. Each one is a public comment under a post, so anyone who sees the post can see your reply.

> [!NOTE]
> Comments only appear once your admin has connected those channels and allowed comments. If the list says "Comments on your Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts appear here once those channels are connected", ask your admin.

## Before you start

You need permission to moderate public comments to reply, hide, or mark comments. Without it you can read comments, but the reply tools and the refresh button do not appear. Deleting comments needs a separate permission that admins have by default. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Read the list

![The Comments tab showing the To do view, source icons, and a list of comments with their post captions](/help/img/social-comments-01-list.png)

- The number on the **Comments** tab is how many comments still wait for a first response.
- Use the pills to change the view: **To do** (waiting for a first response), **Handled** (replied to or resolved), **Spam**, or **All**.
- Use **Search comments…** to find a comment by its text.
- Click **All** or the Facebook, Instagram or TikTok icon to show one source.
- A blue dot and bold name mean the comment is still to do.
- Small tags show more: **Ad post**, **Hidden**, **Deleted**, **Replied**, **Resolved**, and **Sample** (a test comment your admin added to try the screens).
- Press **Load more** at the bottom for older comments.

New Facebook and Instagram comments arrive within seconds. TikTok comments can take a few minutes. To check TikTok now, press the refresh button beside the search box. It says how many new comments it found.

## Reply to a comment

1. Click a comment. It opens on the right with the post, the whole thread and, at the bottom, the reply tools.
2. Make sure **Reply publicly** is selected.
3. Type in the box. It says: "Write a public reply. Everyone who sees the post will see it." A counter shows the limit.
4. Press **Send**, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

The limits are 8,000 characters on Facebook, 2,200 on Instagram and 1,200 on TikTok. After you reply, the comment moves to **Handled** and shows **Replied**.

![One comment open with the post, the thread, the Reply publicly and Private message tabs, and the Hide button](/help/img/social-comments-02-detail.png)

## Send a private message

Choose **Private message** instead. The box says "Write a private message. Only this person will see it."

- You can send **one** private message per comment, within 7 days of the comment.
- After that, the conversation only continues if the person replies.
- TikTok does not allow private messages to people who comment.

## Hide or unhide a comment

Press **Hide** to remove a comment from public view without deleting it. It shows the **Hidden** tag. Press **Unhide** to bring it back.

## Delete a comment

**Delete** is only shown to people allowed to delete comments, which is admins by default. A box asks: "Delete this comment on the platform? This cannot be undone." On TikTok you can only delete your own comments. Hide other people's comments instead.

## Mark a comment handled

At the top of the comment, press:

- **Mark resolved** when nothing more is needed.
- **Spam** to move it to **Spam** without replying.
- **Reopen** to put a resolved or spam comment back in **To do**.

## Why an action can be unavailable

When you cannot do something, the reply area shows the reason instead. The common ones:

- "This comment was deleted, so there is nothing more to do."
- "This is a comment from you or your page."
- "Instagram only lets you reply to a top-level comment. Reply to the first comment in the thread instead."
- "Instagram does not allow replies to a hidden comment. Unhide it first."
- "TikTok has no way to message someone who commented. Reply publicly instead."
- "A private message was already sent for this comment. They can continue the conversation by replying to it."
- "Private messages can only be sent within 7 days of the comment."
- "We do not know who wrote this comment, so we cannot message them."

The comment also lists **Recent actions**, such as "Replied · done", so you can see what has already been done and whether it worked.

## Tips

- If the comment mentions a problem you cannot solve in public, reply briefly in public, then use a private message to ask for details.
- Do not post personal details in a public reply.
- If the customer is already a contact, the header shows "Contact:" and their name.

## Common mistakes

- **Replying in public with private information.** Anyone can read it.
- **Trying to send a second private message.** Only one is allowed per comment.
- **Marking spam by accident.** Use **Reopen** to undo it.
