## The task list

Every channel and DM has its own task list — a simple, standalone to-do list. It is not connected to the Tickets module; use it for internal jobs like "update the deck" or "check with legal", not customer work.

1. Click **Tasks · N open** at the top of a channel.
2. Type in **Add a task…** and press **Add task**.
3. Tick a task to mark it done. It moves from **Open** to **Completed**.

![The Tasks panel with an add-task box, an Open section and a Completed section](/help/img/tasks-and-meetings-01-panel.png)

A task can have an **assignee** and a **due date**. Assigning a task to someone else sends them a notification. When a task is completed, Vircle CRM records who completed it and when — this cannot be edited to say someone else did it.

## Create a task from a message

Point at any message and click **Add to tasks**. The message's text becomes the task's title, and the task keeps a link back to the message it came from.

## Start a meeting

Click **Start a meeting** at the top of a channel. It posts a fresh Google Meet link into the channel for everyone to join — there is no calendar booking yet, just a quick link.

## Tips

- Use the task list for short-lived, internal jobs. If something needs a customer-facing owner, due date tracking and history, raise a Ticket instead.
- Assign a task instead of just mentioning someone — an assignment sends a clear notification, a mention in a busy channel can get lost.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for a Sembang task in Tickets.** They are separate lists; a task is never automatically turned into a ticket.
- **Expecting Start a meeting to book a calendar slot.** It only posts a join link.
