## The task list

Every channel and DM has its own task list — a standalone project-style to-do list, not connected to the Tickets module. Use it for internal jobs like "update the deck" or "check with legal", not customer work.

1. Click the **Tasks** tab at the top of a channel.
2. Type in **Add a task…** and press **Add task**.
3. Tick a task's checkbox to mark it **Done**. Click anywhere else on the row to open its full detail.

![The Tasks tab with an add-task box, an open section and a completed section](/help/img/tasks-and-meetings-01-panel.png)

A task has a **status**, one or more **assignees**, a **due date**, a **description**, and can hold its own **subtasks**. Assigning someone sends them a notification. When a task is marked Done, Halo records who completed it and when — this cannot be edited to say someone else did it.

## Task status

A task moves through three statuses: **To Do**, **In Progress** and **Done**. The row checkbox is a quick done/not-done toggle — to move a task specifically to **In Progress**, open its detail and use the status dropdown there.

## Open a task's details

Click anywhere on a task's row (not the checkbox) to open it. The detail view has two sides:

- **The task itself** — an editable title and description, the status dropdown, a due date, and its assignees.
- **Activity and comments** — a running log of every change (status, assignees, subtasks, due date), and a comment thread with formatting (bold, italic, underline, bulleted and numbered lists) so people can discuss the task without leaving it.

![An open task's detail view, with fields on the left and activity and comments on the right](/help/img/tasks-and-meetings-02-detail.png)

### Assignees

Click the assignees field to add or remove people — a task can have more than one. Each removal or addition shows up in the activity log, so it is clear who was added or dropped and when.

### Subtasks

Break a task into smaller, independently-checkable steps: type into the subtask box and press Enter to add one, tick its checkbox to complete it, and optionally give it its own single assignee. The task row shows a small count, for example "1/3", so you can see progress without opening it. Completing every subtask does **not** automatically move the parent task to Done — that is still your call.

### Raise a ticket from a task

Click the ticket icon on a task row, or **Create Ticket** inside its detail view, to raise a real Ticket from it — useful when what started as an internal job turns out to need customer-facing tracking. Halo opens the new ticket for you as soon as it is created, and the task keeps a link back to it ("View" plus the ticket's key).

## Create a task from a message

Point at any message and click **Add to tasks**. The message's text becomes the task's title, and the task keeps a link back to the message it came from.

## Start a meeting

Click **Start a meeting** at the top of a channel. It posts a fresh Google Meet link into the channel for everyone to join — there is no calendar booking yet, just a quick link.

## Tips

- Use the task list for short-lived, internal jobs. If something needs a customer-facing owner, due date tracking and history, raise a Ticket instead — or turn the task straight into one.
- Assign a task instead of just mentioning someone — an assignment sends a clear notification, a mention in a busy channel can get lost.
- Break a big task into subtasks as soon as you know the steps, rather than one vague task nobody feels they can tick off.
- Use the comment thread on a task for back-and-forth about it, instead of scattering the discussion across the main channel.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for a Sembang task in Tickets.** They are separate lists; a task is never automatically turned into a ticket unless you click Create Ticket on it.
- **Expecting Start a meeting to book a calendar slot.** It only posts a join link.
- **Expecting subtasks to auto-complete the parent task.** They do not — mark the task itself Done separately.
