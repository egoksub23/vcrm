This page covers everything you do with a single message in Sembang, and how threads work.

## Send a message

Type in the box at the bottom of a channel or direct message and press **Enter** (use <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line). You can also:

- **@mention** someone. They get a notification. Type `@` and pick a name from the list.
- **Attach a file** with the paperclip button. Files are stored privately — nobody outside the channel can reach them, even with a direct link. The limit is 16 MB per file.
- **Format your text**: the **Bold**, *Italic* and list buttons in the toolbar wrap or prefix your selection, or you can type `**bold**`, `_italic_` and `- ` for a list yourself.
- **Add a code block**: click the code-fence button, or type three backticks on their own line before and after the code.

![The Sembang composer with the formatting toolbar, attach and code buttons](/help/img/messaging-and-threads-01-composer.png)

## React, pin, edit and remove

Point at a message to see its actions:

- **React** with an emoji. Click a reaction that already has one to add your own, or remove it if it is already yours.
- **Pin** a message so it is easy to find later. Any member can pin. Any member can pin and unpin their own pin; a moderator or admin can unpin anyone's.
- **Star** a message to save it to your own **Starred** list — this is private to you, unlike a pin, which the whole channel sees. See [Search, tasks and notifications](/help/sembang/search-notifications-and-access).
- **Edit** your own message. It shows a small "(edited)" marker.
- **Delete** your own message, or, if you are a moderator or admin, anyone's — except in a direct message, see below.
- **Reply in thread** to start or continue a thread on that message.

## Threads

Click **N replies** under a message, or **Reply in thread**, to open its thread on the right. A thread keeps a side conversation attached to one message instead of cluttering the main channel.

![A thread open on the right, showing the original message and its replies](/help/img/messaging-and-threads-02-thread.png)

When you reply in a thread, tick **Also send to #channel** if you want your reply to also show up in the main channel timeline, not just the thread — useful when the reply matters to everyone, not only people already following the thread. A reply sent this way shows "↪ replied to a thread" with a short preview of the original message, so anyone can click through to the full thread.

### Threads you're in

Click **Threads** at the top of Sembang to see every thread you started, replied to, or were @mentioned in, across every channel and direct message at once — not just the one you have open.

## A note on direct messages

In a direct message, the person who started it does **not** get moderator powers over your messages the way a channel moderator would — nobody can remove a message except its own author. Everything else on this page (reactions, pins, stars, edit, threads) works the same in a DM as in a channel.

## Tips

- Use a thread instead of a burst of short messages when you are working through something with one or two people.
- "Also send to #channel" is for replies the whole channel should see — use it sparingly, or the channel timeline gets noisy again.
- Star a message instead of trying to remember to scroll back to it later.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting a plain reply to appear in the main channel.** It only shows up there if you tick "Also send to #channel".
- **Trying to remove someone else's message in a DM.** You cannot — only the author can remove their own message in a direct message.
- **Looking for a starred message in Pins.** They are separate: Pins are shared with the channel, Stars are private to you.
