## Search everywhere at once

Click the **search** icon at the top of Sembang. Unlike the search box inside a channel (which only looks at that one channel), this searches every channel and direct message you belong to at once. Type at least two characters. Each result shows a snippet and which channel or DM it came from — click one to open that conversation.

![The Search dialog with results from several different channels](/help/img/search-notifications-and-access-01-search.png)

## Starred messages

Click the **star** icon at the top of Sembang to see everything you have starred, across every channel and DM, newest first. This list is private to you — starring a message never tells anyone else. See [Messaging and threads](/help/sembang/messaging-and-threads) for how to star a message.

## Threads you're in

Click **Threads** to see every thread you started, replied to, or were @mentioned in, across the whole of Sembang. See [Messaging and threads](/help/sembang/messaging-and-threads).

## Member directory

Click **Directory** to see everyone in your account who currently has access to Sembang — not just people who happen to share a channel with you already. Useful for finding someone to start a channel or DM with.

## Mute a channel or DM

Open its menu and choose **Mute channel**. A muted conversation stops sending you notifications entirely — mentions included. It still shows in your channel list with a small muted icon, and you can read it any time; you just will not be pinged. Choose **Unmute channel** to turn notifications back on.

## What notifies you

- Someone **@mentions** you in a channel you belong to.
- **Any message** in a direct message, whether or not you are mentioned — a DM is a direct conversation, so it always notifies unless you mute it.
- Someone **assigns you a task**.

All of these are switched off for a channel or DM you have muted. Notifications arrive the same way as everywhere else in Vircle CRM — see [Notifications](/help/working-together/notifications).

## Who can see what

- **Sembang itself** is switched on per role. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
- **A public channel** is visible to search, in Browse channels, and to anyone with Sembang access once they join.
- **A private channel or a DM** is only visible to its members, plus account admins.
- **The member directory and cross-channel search** only show what you already have access to — search never surfaces a private channel you are not in.

## Tips

- Mute a busy channel instead of leaving it — you keep read access and history, you just stop getting pinged.
- Search is often faster than scrolling, especially in an old channel with a lot of history.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting a mute to hide the channel.** It still appears in your list; only notifications stop.
- **Searching for something in a channel you have not joined.** Cross-channel search only looks at conversations you can already see.
