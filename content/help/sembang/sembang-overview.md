Sembang is your team's own chat, built into Vircle CRM. Use it to talk with colleagues, run projects and keep quick notes — separate from the Inbox, which is only for customers. Nothing you write in Sembang is ever visible to a customer, and nothing from the Inbox appears in Sembang.

## Who can see Sembang

By default, **Sembang is switched on for Owners and Admins only.** If you do not see **Sembang** in the sidebar, ask an admin to grant it to your role from **Settings**, **Permissions**. This is different from most other menus, which every role can see by default — Sembang is opt-in on purpose, since it is your team's private space. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

Once you have access to Sembang at all, what you can actually open still depends on channel membership: a private channel or a direct message is only visible to the people in it, plus admins.

## The layout

Click **Sembang** in the sidebar. On a computer you see two panes:

1. **The channel list**, on the left — your channels, then your direct messages, each showing the last message and an unread count.
2. **The open conversation**, on the right — the messages, and the reply box at the bottom.

![Sembang with the channel list on the left and an open channel on the right](/help/img/sembang-overview-01-layout.png)

On a phone or small tablet you see one pane at a time. Tap a channel to open it, and use the back arrow at the top left to return to the list.

At the top of the open conversation you also have a global **Search** button and a **Starred** button that work across every channel and DM at once, not just the one you have open. See [Search, tasks and notifications](/help/sembang/search-notifications-and-access).

## Create a channel

1. Click **+** at the top of the channel list.
2. Type a **name**. Channel names are unique per workspace.
3. Choose **Public** (anyone with Sembang access can see and join it) or **Private** (only people you invite).
4. For a private channel, tick the people to invite.
5. Click **Create**.

You become the channel's **moderator** automatically. A moderator can remove messages and, for a private channel, add or remove members. Account admins can see and moderate every channel, public or private, whether or not they are a member.

## Join a public channel

Public channels only used to appear once you were invited. Now there is a proper way to find them:

1. In the channel list, click **Browse channels**.
2. You see every public channel in your workspace that you have not already joined, with its topic and how many members it has.
3. Click **Join** next to the one you want.

![The Browse channels list with a Join button next to each channel](/help/img/sembang-overview-02-browse.png)

## Channel topic

A moderator or admin can click the topic line at the top of an open channel to edit it. Everyone in the channel sees the change.

## Archive a channel

A moderator or admin can archive a channel from its menu. An archived channel disappears from everyone's channel list, but nothing is deleted. To bring one back, open **Archived channels** from the channel list and click **Unarchive**.

## Tips

- Keep one topic per channel. A channel called *#payments-team* is easier to search than a catch-all *#general* full of unrelated threads.
- Use a private channel for anything sensitive. Remember that account admins can always see it.
- If you cannot find a channel you know exists, check **Browse channels** — it may be public and you just have not joined it yet.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for Sembang and not finding it.** It is switched off for your role by default. Ask an admin.
- **Expecting a private channel to be invisible to admins.** It is not — admins can see and moderate every channel.
- **Assuming Browse channels shows private channels too.** It only lists public ones you have not joined.
