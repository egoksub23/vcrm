Some changes are checked by a second person before everyone can use them. You propose, and a reviewer approves. This keeps the tags, labels, snippets and articles your whole team uses tidy and correct.

## What "pending" means

When you propose something, it is saved as **Pending**. Only you and the reviewers can see it. It is left out of every list, search, automation and broadcast until a reviewer approves it. You cannot use it yet.

## What needs approval

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Create a **tag** or a **conversation label**, or change an existing one | It is sent for approval. |
| Create or change a **snippet** (a saved reply) | If your admin switched on approval for snippets, it is sent for approval. If not, it saves at once. |
| Save a **knowledge article** | It is saved as a draft. A reviewer approves it, which publishes it. |
| **Apply** an existing tag or label to a chat or a contact | No approval. It is free. |

Nothing changes for what already exists. Old tags, labels and snippets keep working for everyone.

## Propose a tag or a label

1. Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Tags** or **Conversation labels**.
2. Click **Create tag** or **Create label**.
3. Type a name and, if you like, a description. Choose a colour.
4. Click **Send for approval**. You see "Sent for approval".

To change an existing one, open its edit window and click **Send for approval** again. The current version stays live until the reviewer decides.

For snippets, open **Settings**, then **Quick replies**. The button says **Send for approval** when approval is switched on for you.

> [!NOTE]
> The list in the chat reply box is called **Snippets**. The list in Settings is called **Quick replies**. It is the same thing.

## What you see on your own items

| Chip | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for a reviewer. You cannot use it yet. |
| **Pending changes** | Your edit waits for a reviewer. The current version stays as it is. |
| **Rejected** | A reviewer said no. Point at the chip to read the note. |
| **Changes rejected** | The edit was rejected. The old version is still live. |

You can withdraw a proposal that is still pending. For a rejected one, you can dismiss it, or edit it and send it again.

## What a reviewer sees

Reviewers open **Settings**, then **Approvals**, with the tabs **Pending** and **Decided**. For each item they choose **Approve**, **Edit then approve** or **Reject**. A rejection needs a note of at least 3 characters, so you can read why. A reviewer cannot decide their own proposal. Another reviewer must.

By default the Owner and Admins are the reviewers. If you cannot see **Approvals** in Settings, that is normal for an Agent.

## Who tells you what happened

You get a notification when a reviewer decides. Click it to go to the list where you proposed the item. See [Notifications](/help/working-together/notifications).

## Tips

- Use a clear name and description. It makes the review quick.
- Search the existing tags and labels before you propose a new one. A name that already exists is refused.
- If a proposal waits a long time, ask a reviewer in person.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting for a Pending tag to appear in the chat's tag list. It is not there until it is approved.
- Expecting to approve your own proposal.
- Proposing a second tag that means the same as one that exists.
