A broadcast sends one approved WhatsApp template message to many contacts at once, for example a reminder or an announcement. This page keeps it short. A broadcast reaches many customers in one go, so it needs care.

## Who sends broadcasts

Your admin decides who may send them. By default the **Send broadcasts** permission is given to Agents, but many teams keep broadcasts for a manager. Ask your team lead before you send one. If the **New Broadcast** button is greyed out, your role cannot send broadcasts. A Viewer can look at broadcasts but not create them. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

To message one customer, do not use a broadcast. Send a template from the chat instead. See [Reply to customers](/help/inbox/reply-to-customers).

## What you can see

Click **Broadcasts** in the sidebar. The list shows each broadcast's Name, Template, Recipients, Delivery, Read, Status and Date. The status is Draft, Scheduled, Sending, Sent or Failed.

![The Broadcasts list with the name, template, delivery and status columns.](/help/img/broadcasts-01-list.png)

Click a broadcast to see how it went. You get totals for Total Recipients, Sent, Delivered, Read, Replied and Failed, a funnel, and a table of every recipient with its status and any error. Use the status filter to see only the ones that failed. **Export CSV** saves the recipient list.

## What a sender does

A new broadcast has four steps: **Template**, **Audience**, **Personalize** and **Send**.

1. Choose an approved template.
2. Choose the audience: **All Contacts**, **Filter by Tags**, **Custom Field** or **Upload CSV**.
3. Fill in the template's variables, for example the customer's name.
4. Give the broadcast a name, and click **Save as Draft** to come back later, or **Send Broadcast**.

Before it sends, a **Confirm Broadcast** window shows how many contacts will receive it and warns: "This action cannot be undone."

> [!WARNING]
> Check the number of contacts in the **Confirm Broadcast** window. A broadcast cannot be taken back once it is sent.

## When a broadcast stops half-way

Keep the browser tab open while it says **Sending...**. If the tab is closed too early, some recipients are never sent. On the broadcast's page you will see a box called **This campaign stopped part-way**. Click **Resume sending** to finish it. If some recipients failed, click **Retry failed**.

You can delete a broadcast, but not while it is sending.

## Replies

When a customer replies to a broadcast, the reply arrives in the Inbox like any other chat. Answer it in the usual way. See [Inbox overview](/help/inbox/inbox-overview).

## Tips

- Try a new template on a very small audience first, if your team allows it.
- Read the template on the Personalize step. Check that no variable is empty.
- Ask a teammate to check the audience before you send.

## Common mistakes

- Sending to All Contacts when you meant a tag group.
- Closing the tab while it is still sending.
- Using a broadcast for a single customer.
