Notifications tell you when a teammate hands you work or asks for your help, and when a promised time is close. You do not need to watch every screen.

## Open your notifications

Click **Notifications** in the sidebar. It has a bell icon. A number on the bell counts the notifications you have not read yet. It shows **9+** when there are more than nine. The **Inbox** item has its own dot for unread chats, which is separate.

![The Notifications page with unread notifications marked by a dot.](/help/img/notifications-01-page.png)

The page lists your latest 100 notifications, newest first. An unread one has a highlighted background and a small dot. Each has a title, a short line of detail and the time.

## Read and clear

1. Click a notification. It opens the place it is about: the chat, the ticket, or the page where you can act. It is also marked as read.
2. To clear all of them, click **Mark all as read**.

New notifications appear without you refreshing the page.

## What sends you a notification

| You get one when | Where it takes you |
|---|---|
| A teammate assigns you a chat ("New conversation assigned") | The chat |
| A teammate @mentions you in an internal comment in a chat ("You were mentioned") | The chat |
| A teammate assigns you a ticket ("New ticket assigned") | The ticket |
| A teammate @mentions you, or a team you are in, in a ticket comment | The ticket, at that comment |
| A teammate nudges you because they still wait for your response on a ticket | The ticket, at that comment |
| Something changes on a ticket you watch: the status, the assignee, or a new comment | The ticket |
| A ticket's first response or resolution time is at risk or breached | The ticket |
| A customer has waited too long for a reply ("Response time exceeded"). It goes to the assigned agent, or to the admins if nobody is assigned. | The chat |
| The Jira issue linked to your ticket is Done | The ticket |
| A reviewer decides something you proposed | The page where you proposed it |
| You are a reviewer and someone proposed something | The Approvals page |

A ticket notification about a mention is marked as read for you when the request is closed: you replied, you clicked **Mark as done**, the person withdrew it, or the ticket was resolved.

You are never notified about something you did yourself. If you assign a chat or a ticket to yourself, you get no notification.

## The bubble on Tickets

Separate from the bell, the **Tickets** item in the sidebar has a round number too. It counts the tickets where a teammate asked you for a response, by @mentioning you or your team, and it is still open. It shows **9+** above nine and is hidden at zero. It is there as soon as you sign in and updates on its own. On the Tickets page, **Mentioned me** lists those tickets. See [Work a ticket](/help/tickets/work-a-ticket) for how a request is closed.

## Alerts in your browser

The app can also show a desktop alert for each new customer message, even when you are on another page. It is off until you switch it on. It works only while the app is open in a browser tab, and the setting is saved on that device only.

1. Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Your profile** in the list on the left.
2. Find **Browser notifications**.
3. Turn on **Notify me about new customer messages**.
4. Allow notifications when your browser asks.
5. Click **Send test notification** to check.

If it says "Blocked in browser settings", open your browser's site settings (usually the lock icon next to the address bar), allow notifications for this site, then reload the page.

## Tips

- Check **Notifications** when you start your shift and after lunch.
- Use **Watch** on tickets you care about, but do not watch everything.
- Turn on browser alerts if you often work in another tab.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting for a notification about your own change. You never get one.
- Leaving notifications unread for days. The bell number then means nothing.
- Expecting browser alerts on a second computer. Turn them on for each device.
