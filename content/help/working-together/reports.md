Reports show how your whole workspace is doing: how many chats came in, how fast you answer, and how tickets are going. Use them to see whether your team is keeping up.

## Open a report

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Click one of the tabs at the top.
3. At the top right, choose the date range: **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **This month**. It starts on Last 7 days.

Most tiles compare with the period just before, and say "vs previous period".

![The Tickets report with the summary tiles, the backlog by age and the SLA compliance section.](/help/img/reports-01-tickets.png)

By default every role can open Reports. The numbers cover the whole workspace, not only your own work.

## What each tab shows

| Tab | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Conversations** | How many conversations were Opened and Closed each day. |
| **Responses** | The average time to a first response. Lower is better. |
| **Resolutions** | The average time from open to closed. |
| **Messages** | Incoming and outgoing messages each day. |
| **Contacts** | New contacts each day. |
| **Assignments** | Conversations created, split by agent and by team. |
| **Leaderboard** | Agents ranked by messages sent, with conversations assigned, conversations closed and average response time. |
| **Users** | The same figures for each teammate. It is not a history of who logged in. |
| **Lifecycle** | How many contacts are Lead, Active, Customer or Churned now, and how many moved into each stage in the period. |
| **Broadcasts** | Broadcasts sent, total recipients, and the delivered, read and failed rates. |
| **Tickets** | Tickets open now, opened and resolved, average first response and resolution time, tickets resolved within 24 hours, the backlog by age, breakdowns and SLA compliance. |

## How to read them

- **First response** on the Responses tab is the time from a customer's message to the next reply in that chat. The reply can be from a person or from the AI assistant. A ticket's first response is different. It is the time from creating the ticket to the first comment by a teammate.
- **A change against the last period** is only a comparison. For times, a smaller number is better.
- **Resolutions** for chats closed before 17 September 2026 use an approximate close time, so treat old numbers with care.
- **Leaderboard and Users** count messages sent from your own login. Older activity may be undercounted.
- **Lifecycle** counts a contact once, under its latest stage.
- **Tickets, Open backlog by age** shows what is open right now. It ignores the date range. Its groups are Under 1 day, 1 to 3 days, 3 to 7 days and 7+ days.
- **Tickets, SLA compliance** counts tickets created in the period that have an SLA. Compliance is met divided by met plus breached. Tickets that are still on the clock are not counted for or against it. A list of tickets that missed a target is shown below.

## Tips

- Compare the same range each week, such as Last 7 days on Monday.
- Look at **Tickets** before a team meeting. The backlog by age shows what is being left behind.
- Change the range before you decide a number is wrong. Today is often too short to judge.

## Common mistakes

- Reading the Leaderboard as a score of quality. It counts messages, not how good they were.
- Comparing a full week with a half week.
- Looking for your own numbers only. These reports are for the workspace.
