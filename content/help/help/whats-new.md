This page lists what changed in each release, in plain words, newest first. It only includes things you can see or use as an agent. Some features need your admin to switch them on.

## 0.49.0 · 21 September 2026

- **The web chat widget looks like WhatsApp.** Visitors see bubbles, sent, delivered and read ticks, times, an emoji picker and voice notes. They can send text, voice, emoji, pictures, video and files. You can send the same back from the Inbox.
- **New badges on web chats:** **Verified in-app** (your own app proved who the visitor is), **Unverified web claim** (the visitor typed a phone or email) and **Possible duplicate**. A possible duplicate is never merged on its own. You choose **Merge** or **Dismiss**.
- **Web enquiries become leads.** A visitor who is not signed in can say "I'm an existing user" or "I'm enquiring". Enquiries arrive as leads tagged *Web enquiry*.

See [Web chat conversations](/help/inbox/web-chat-conversations) and [Merge duplicates](/help/contacts/merge-duplicates).

## 0.48.0 · 21 September 2026

- **AI steps in automations.** Your admin can now let the AI answer, sort or summarise a chat before it reaches you. Some chats may arrive with an AI note or a hand-off.
- **A ticket can be created for you.** When a chat is closed, an automation can open a ticket, and the AI can write its subject and description.

## 0.47.0 · 21 September 2026

- **Emoji everywhere you type.** The chat box has a smiley button. Type `:` and two letters, such as `:smi`, to see suggestions. The same works in ticket comments and descriptions, notes, closing notes, snippets and knowledge articles.

## 0.46.0 · 21 September 2026

- **Buttons you cannot use are switched off, with an "Ask an admin" hint.** You will see this in settings, contacts, deals, pipelines, the inbox, broadcasts, automations, knowledge and AI screens. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
- **Your proposed edits are private.** Only you and the reviewers can read the values you propose. See [Approvals](/help/working-together/approvals).

## 0.45.0 · 20 September 2026

- **More Jira features.** Send a ticket's files to Jira, and see files that came from Jira. In the ticket list, create Jira issues or link many tickets at once. See [Jira link](/help/tickets/jira-link).

## 0.44.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Ticket SLA with business hours.** A ticket can carry a promised time. A badge shows **On track**, **At risk**, **Breached**, **Paused** or **Met**, with a countdown, on board cards, list rows and the ticket.
- **New ticket filters:** **SLA at risk** and **SLA breached**. You can also sort and group by SLA.
- **New notifications** when a ticket is at risk and when it is breached.
- **Reports, then Tickets** now shows SLA compliance.

See [Work a ticket](/help/tickets/work-a-ticket).

## 0.43.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Link tickets to Jira.** Create a Jira issue from a ticket, or link an existing one. You see its live status on the ticket, and you can share an internal note with Jira.

## 0.42.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Propose and approve.** You can propose new tags, labels and snippets, and changes to them. A reviewer approves them. Your proposals show as **Pending**. An agent's knowledge article is a draft that a reviewer approves.
- **Contact imports by an agent skip unknown tags** instead of creating them.

See [Approvals](/help/working-together/approvals).

## 0.40.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Deleted items can be restored.** Articles, tags, labels and snippets that are deleted are kept for 90 days, and an admin can restore them. This does not apply to contacts.

## 0.39.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Tickets look and work like a project board.** Every ticket has a key such as **VIR-12**. The board has the columns Open, In progress, Pending, Resolved and Closed. Drag a card to change its status.
- **A List view** with editing inside the row, sorting, grouping and bulk actions.
- **Search, quick filters and saved filters.** Filters are in the page address, so you can share a link.
- **Due dates and labels** on tickets. **Watching**: the person who raised a ticket and its assignee watch it automatically.
- **A better New Ticket form** with **Create another**.

See [Tickets overview](/help/tickets/tickets-overview).

## 0.38.0 and 0.38.1 · 20 September 2026

- **Paste a picture into a knowledge article** with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>. Pictures travel with the answer.
- **Paste or drop a picture into a chat message.** It shows as a small chip above the reply box.
- **Voice notes: choose the microphone**, with a level meter and a "No sound detected" warning.

## 0.37.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Roles follow the settings your admin chooses.** Menus and buttons can be different for each role. A hidden menu shows a friendly "no access" page. See [Roles and permissions](/help/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## 0.36.0 · 20 September 2026

- **Knowledge translations.** Translate an article into Bahasa Melayu or Chinese with AI. The result is a draft you check. An **Out of date** warning shows when the original changes. See [Translations](/help/knowledge/translations).

## 0.35.0 · 20 September 2026

- **A full article editor** with formatting, attachments, edit history and a test box: "Test it: would the AI find this?".
- **Collections, Add content and Unanswered questions.** Bring in Q&A pairs, files and web pages as drafts.
- **Knowledge in the chat.** The **Knowledge** tab, `/kb` and **Insert**. AI drafts show **Based on** chips. See [Use articles in a chat](/help/knowledge/use-articles-in-chat).

## 0.34.0 to 0.34.3 · 20 September 2026

- **AI closing notes and summaries.** Closing a chat can draft the note and suggest a label. **Summarise this conversation** writes a short summary. Always check both.
- **Email replies work like Outlook.** The reply box stays on top and the quoted email sits below.
- **Snippets and Knowledge slide up** over the chat instead of replacing the reply box.
- **A wider inbox list.** The Comments tab is fully visible.

## 0.33.0 · 20 September 2026

- **The Comments tab.** Public comments on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are in the Inbox. You can reply, send a private message, hide, delete (admins) and resolve. See [Social comments](/help/inbox/social-comments).

## 0.32.0 · 20 September 2026

- **The Knowledge base has its own page** for agents and the AI. Articles come in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese. Use **Add to knowledge base** on a message.

## 0.31.0 · 19 September 2026

- **Session notes show who wrote them and when.** You can edit or delete your own notes. Admins can change anyone's.

## 0.29.0 to 0.30.1 · 19 September 2026

- **Ticket history and notes sit beside the chat.**
- **Edit the contact in the right-hand column.** Click a name, phone, email or company. Choose a country and a language. The language is used by AI drafts and replies.

## 0.28.0 · 19 September 2026

- **Tags and conversation labels are separate.** Tag colours show in the chat list and thread. Add or remove tags and labels from the right-hand column.

## 0.27.0 · 19 September 2026

- **Bulk actions in the Inbox.** Assign, close, mark read or apply a label to many chats at once. See [Views and bulk actions](/help/inbox/views-and-bulk-actions).

## 0.26.0 to 0.26.1 · 19 September 2026

- **Chats and Emails tabs** in the Inbox.
- **Closed and reopened markers** in the chat, including "Reopened, customer wrote again".
- **A fuller Tickets report.**

## 0.24.0 to 0.25.0 · 19 September 2026

- **Closing a chat needs a note.** Every chat has a session log of who did what.
- **Ticket history log** and a **customisable ticket form** with extra fields.

## 0.23.0 · 19 September 2026

- **Tickets arrive.** Raise a ticket from a contact or from a chat.
- **Contact merge** when a phone number matches another contact.
- **Rich email replies** and **Move to trash** for a message.

## Earlier

Earlier releases added the channels: WhatsApp, the web chat, Messenger, Instagram, Gmail and Microsoft 365 email.
