Halo Sign: screens and flows

What each person sees, in the order they meet it. These are wireframes in Halo's own layout, not final visuals: they fix what is on each screen and what each control does, so the build matches what you approved. Numbers on a screen match the notes beside it. Requirement ids (F-12 and so on) refer to document 1.

Reading tip. Violet marks the first signer's fields and teal the second's, everywhere in this document, the same way they appear in the editor and on the signing page. Amber means something the signer still has to do. All screens work on a phone for the signer; the sender's screens are designed for a computer first and stack on a tablet.

Principles and map

PrincipleWhat it means on screen
The signer is a stranger on a phoneNo login, no jargon, one thing per screen, a visible Next. Thumb-reachable buttons at the bottom.
The sender starts from the contactMost documents are sent from a contact page; nothing is typed that Halo already knows.
Status is always visibleEvery list and detail screen answers "where is it, who is it waiting on" without opening anything.
Nothing silentEvery action says what happened (a toast, an audit line). Destructive actions ask once and ask for a reason.
Proof is a featureThe audit trail, the certificate page and "audit trail intact" are shown, not hidden in a menu.
Neutral wordingCustomer-facing pages say the workspace's name and "Halo"; the supplier's name never appears.

Sender-side empty states and errors

SituationWhat the screen says and offers
No documents yet"Nothing sent yet. Send your first document from a contact or from here." Button: New document.
No templates yet"Upload a PDF to make your first template." Button: Upload PDF. Link: how templates work.
Upload fails (password, corrupt, too big)Names the problem and the limit: "This PDF is password protected. Remove the password and upload it again."
Recipient email bouncesDocument appears under Needs attention; the signer row shows "Email not delivered. Change recipient."
Monthly limit reachedSend button replaced by "Your workspace has used all 200 documents this month." with the limit and who to contact.
Sealing failed"We could not finish the signed file. It will retry; nothing is lost." A Retry button for Admins.
Download blocked (fingerprint mismatch)"This file does not match its record, so it was not downloaded." Raised to Admins as an alert.

Responsive and accessibility rules

AreaRule
Signing pageDesigned at 360 px wide first. Touch targets at least 44 px. Pinch zoom allowed. Works to 200 percent zoom without sideways scroll.
KeyboardEvery control reachable and visibly focused; Next required field on Enter; editor fields movable by arrow keys.
Screen readersEvery field has a label and a "required" state; progress is announced; errors are announced and linked to the field. Typed signature is an alternative to drawing.
ColourStatus is never colour alone: always a word. Contrast meets WCAG AA in light and dark.
LanguageAll text comes from the four Halo language files; no text baked into images; dates and numbers follow the signer's language.
Sender screensComputer first; lists stack to cards on a tablet; the template editor asks to be opened on a larger screen on a phone.

For your review

Please mark anything you want changed by its id or screen name (S1, T2, signer screen 3). The questions that most change the build:

  1. Merchant agreement fields (S2 step 3). Which fields must the merchant fill and which do we know in advance? Which are sensitive?
  2. Countersign. Does the director sign after the merchant (as drawn), before, or both at once?
  3. Code by default? Should the verification code be on for every merchant document, or only when you choose?
  4. Name in the sidebar. "Sign" as drawn, or "Documents"?
  5. Signer's download. Allowed at any time (as drawn), or only after completion?
  6. Language of the first template. English, Bahasa Melayu, or both templates side by side?
  7. Signing page look. Workspace logo and name only (as drawn), or a colour as well?
  8. Anything missing from the screens: a view you expected, a button you would press that is not here.

Wireframes only. Visual design, icons and exact spacing follow Halo's existing components when built.
